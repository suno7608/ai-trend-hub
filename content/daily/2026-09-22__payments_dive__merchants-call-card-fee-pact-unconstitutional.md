<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Walmart and other major retailers are challenging a swipe-fee settlement, arguing that being compelled to remain in a class action violates their constitutional due process rights. The dispute reflects long-standing retail industry grievances over credit card interchange fees. The outcome of this legal challenge could have sweeping implications for payment fee structures across U.S. commerce.

- Walmart and other retailers argue the card-fee settlement class structure violates due process rights
- The case centers on whether merchants can be compelled to remain in a swipe-fee settlement class
- Outcome could materially affect credit card interchange fee structures for U.S. retailers
- Broader implications for D2C brands managing payment processing costs
