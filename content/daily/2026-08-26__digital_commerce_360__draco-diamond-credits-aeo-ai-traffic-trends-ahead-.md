캐나다 기반 랩그로운 다이아몬드 판매업체 Draco Diamond는 2025년 사이버 5 기간 동안 광고 지출 대비 수익(ROAS)이 2% 증가하는 데 그쳤다고 밝혔다. 창업자 Garrett McMartin은 이 수치가 사이버 5 효과로 단정 짓기 어렵다고 언급하며, AI 트래픽 및 AEO(Answer Engine Optimization) 전략을 주목하고 있다고 전했다. 해당 기업은 AI 기반 검색 트렌드에 대응해 선제적으로 마케팅 전략을 조정하고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Canada-based lab-grown diamond retailer Draco Diamond reported only a 2% increase in return on ad spend during the 2025 Cyber 5 period, which founder Garrett McMartin said was too small to attribute solely to the shopping event. The company is focusing on Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and AI-driven traffic trends as key strategic priorities. Draco Diamond is proactively adjusting its marketing approach to align with the rise of AI-powered search and discovery.

</div>

## Key Points

- Draco Diamond recorded only a 2% ROAS increase during Cyber 5 2025, making it difficult to isolate the event's impact
- The company is prioritizing AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) as AI-powered search reshapes discovery
- AI traffic trends are becoming a key consideration for e-commerce marketing planning ahead of peak seasons
