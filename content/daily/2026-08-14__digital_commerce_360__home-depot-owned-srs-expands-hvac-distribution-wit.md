<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Home Depot-owned SRS Distribution has agreed to acquire Lohmiller & Company, a Denver-based wholesale HVAC distributor operating four locations in Colorado, marking SRS's second HVAC-focused acquisition. The deal is part of Home Depot's broader strategy to expand its professional distribution network through SRS. Financial terms of the acquisition were not disclosed.

</div>

## Key Points

- SRS Distribution (Home Depot subsidiary) acquires Lohmiller & Company, a Colorado-based HVAC wholesaler
- This is SRS's second HVAC acquisition, reflecting Home Depot's push into professional distribution
- Deal terms were undisclosed; SRS was founded in 2008
