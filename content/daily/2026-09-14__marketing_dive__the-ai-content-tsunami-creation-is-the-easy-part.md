AI가 콘텐츠 제작을 손쉽게 만들면서, 기업들은 생성된 콘텐츠를 충분히 검토하지 못하는 '검토 병목' 현상에 직면하고 있다. 콘텐츠 생산 속도는 AI 덕분에 급속히 높아졌지만, 품질 관리와 감수 프로세스는 이를 따라가지 못하는 상황이다. 이는 콘텐츠 전략에서 생산량보다 거버넌스와 품질 관리 체계의 중요성이 더욱 커지고 있음을 시사한다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

AI has made content creation effortless, but companies are now overwhelmed by the volume of content they cannot review quickly enough. The speed of AI-generated output has far outpaced existing quality control and editorial review processes. This dynamic signals that content governance and oversight infrastructure have become the true bottleneck in AI-driven marketing operations.

</div>

## Key Points

- AI has dramatically lowered the barrier to content creation, causing a volume surge
- Review and quality assurance processes are the critical bottleneck, not content generation
- Companies face brand and compliance risks from publishing unreviewed AI-generated content
- Content governance infrastructure is now a strategic competitive differentiator
