<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Beauty brand e.l.f. launched 'The e.l.f.ing Magical Sisters,' a Halloween campaign produced by its in-house entertainment arm. The campaign draws on Y2K nostalgia, evoking the feel of the TV show 'Charmed' to connect with Gen Z consumers. The initiative reflects e.l.f.'s strategy of blending brand identity with entertainment content through in-house production.
