Bed Bath & Beyond has launched a new store concept that revisits familiar elements of its original format, including the iconic towel wall and coupons. The move comes as its parent company reported strong revenue gains driven by recent acquisitions. The strategy appears aimed at recapturing brand nostalgia while repositioning in the retail market.
