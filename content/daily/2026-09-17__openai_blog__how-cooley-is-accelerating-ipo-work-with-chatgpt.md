Law firm Cooley has developed 'GO Public,' an AI-powered platform built with ChatGPT designed to streamline the IPO process for lawyers. The tool helps legal professionals surface potential issues earlier and concentrate their expertise on high-judgment decisions. This case illustrates how specialized AI applications are transforming complex professional workflows in the legal sector.
