<div class="lang-ko">

AI 검색 결과가 빠르게 변화함에 따라 생성형 엔진 최적화(GEO)는 이제 주 단위로 관리해야 하는 상시 업무가 됐다. PR, SEO, 콘텐츠 팀이 구매자의 질문, 인용 소스 변화, 주간 성과를 공유하는 통합 뷰가 필요하다고 강조된다. AI 답변의 변동성이 높아질수록 브랜드가 AI 추천 결과에 포함되기 위한 전략적 노력이 더욱 중요해지고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) has evolved from a periodic task into a weekly operational discipline as AI-generated search answers change rapidly and unpredictably. PR, SEO, and content teams now need a unified, shared view of what questions buyers are asking, which sources AI systems cite, and how these shift week over week. As AI answer volatility increases, brands must continuously adapt their content strategy to remain visible in AI-generated responses.

</div>

## Key Points

- GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) now requires weekly monitoring and adjustment, not quarterly SEO reviews
- PR, SEO, and content teams must share a unified dashboard tracking buyer questions, cited sources, and weekly shifts
- AI search answer volatility means brand visibility in AI responses can change significantly week to week
- Organizations that treat GEO as a continuous process will have a structural advantage over those with static content strategies
