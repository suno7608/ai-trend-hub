푸마와 머스크가 북미 유통 파트너십을 확대하며, 머스크가 푸마의 북미 유통 네트워크 전반을 관리하게 됐다. 머스크는 애리조나, 캘리포니아, 인디애나에 위치한 자동화 창고를 포함해 200만 평방피트 이상의 유통 시설을 운영 중이다. 두 회사의 협력은 해양 운송을 넘어 풀필먼트 전반으로 확장됐다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

A.P. Moller-Maersk has expanded its partnership with Puma to manage the sportswear brand's entire North American distribution network. The logistics giant now operates over 2 million square feet of Puma's distribution infrastructure, including automated warehouses in Arizona, California, and Indiana. The partnership has evolved well beyond ocean shipping into end-to-end fulfillment management.

</div>

## Key Points

- Maersk now manages Puma's full North American distribution network including automated warehouses
- Over 2 million sq ft of distribution infrastructure operated across three U.S. states
- Partnership has expanded from ocean shipping to end-to-end fulfillment
- Demonstrates trend of brands outsourcing logistics to specialized global operators
