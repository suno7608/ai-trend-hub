<div class="lang-ko">

최근 Glossy & Modern Retail Brand Leaders Dinner에서 리테일 경영진들은 AI를 활용한 운영 자동화, 채용 재편, 내부 지식 플랫폼 구축, 마케팅 전략 정교화 사례를 공유했다. 참석자들은 AI 도입이 빠르게 확산되고 있음에도 불구하고, 궁극적으로 경쟁 우위는 AI가 대체할 수 없는 커뮤니티·오프라인 경험 투자에서 나온다고 입을 모았다. 즉, 미래 리테일의 승자는 AI로 효율을 높이면서도 인간적 경험을 강화하는 하이브리드 전략을 택하는 기업이 될 것이다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

At a recent Glossy & Modern Retail Brand Leaders Dinner, founders and executives shared how AI is being used to automate operations, reshape hiring, build internal knowledge platforms, and refine marketing strategies. Despite rapid AI adoption across the industry, attendees agreed that the businesses best positioned to win are those that also invest in community, physical retail, and experiences AI cannot replicate. The emerging retail operating model thus combines leaner, AI-augmented teams with differentiated human-centric experiences.

</div>

## Key Points

- AI is actively being deployed for operational automation, hiring decisions, and internal knowledge management in retail
- Marketing strategy refinement via AI is a key focus among brand leaders
- Physical community and in-person experiences remain critical differentiators that AI cannot replace
- The winning model combines lean AI-powered operations with human-centric brand experiences
