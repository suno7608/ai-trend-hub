<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Wendy's has hired Tariq Hassan, a former McDonald's CMO, as its new Chief Marketing and Customer Growth Officer. Hassan will succeed Lindsay Radkoski, who has served as U.S. CMO since 2023. The appointment is part of Wendy's ongoing turnaround strategy aimed at revitalizing brand performance and customer engagement.
