<div class="lang-ko">

홈 인테리어 D2C 브랜드 Ruggable은 지난해 8월부터 전체 광고비의 약 10%를 Meta 등 퍼포먼스 채널에서 인플루언서 및 커넥티드 TV 등 브랜드 채널로 전환했다. CMO Lauren Sherman-Kaoud는 내년에 이 비율을 15~20% 추가로 확대할 계획이라고 밝혔다. ShopMy 플랫폼을 통한 크리에이터 협업이 새로운 핵심 마케팅 전략으로 자리 잡고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

D2C home décor brand Ruggable has shifted approximately 10% of its total ad spend away from performance channels like Meta toward brand channels such as influencers and connected television since August of last year. Chief Marketing and Creative Officer Lauren Sherman-Kaoud announced plans to increase that shift by an additional 15–20% next year. The ShopMy platform is emerging as a central vehicle for Ruggable's creator-led marketing strategy.

</div>

## Key Points

- Ruggable shifted ~10% of ad spend from Meta performance channels to influencers and connected TV
- Plans to grow this brand channel shift by an additional 15–20% next year
- ShopMy is the key creator platform driving Ruggable's influencer marketing strategy
- Reflects a broader D2C trend of moving from performance marketing toward brand-building channels
