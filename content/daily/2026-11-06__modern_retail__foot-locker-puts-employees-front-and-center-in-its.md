Foot Locker launched its holiday campaign 'Joy to the Sole,' placing employees at the forefront of its seasonal marketing efforts. The campaign signals a new marketing direction for the retailer as it seeks to drive growth through authentic brand storytelling. By featuring frontline employees as brand ambassadors, Foot Locker aims to build more genuine connections with consumers.
