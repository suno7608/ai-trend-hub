<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Alibaba.com has launched the Accio Sourcing Toolkit, a new plugin within its Accio Work platform that enables agentic AI to autonomously find suppliers, compare offers, and manage negotiations around the clock without manual follow-up. The tool is designed to significantly reduce the effort required by e-commerce sellers in the B2B sourcing process. This development marks a major step in the integration of agentic AI into real-world B2B commerce workflows.

</div>

- Signals growing maturity of agentic AI in real commercial procurement workflows
