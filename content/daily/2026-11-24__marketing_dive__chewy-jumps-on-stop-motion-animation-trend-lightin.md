Pet supply retailer Chewy has launched a holiday ad campaign using stop-motion animation, positioning the craft technique as a deliberate counterpoint to the surge of AI-generated creative content. The move reflects a growing trend among brands seeking differentiation through human-crafted aesthetics during the competitive holiday advertising season. This signals that consumer receptivity to handcrafted, analog-feeling creative may be rising as AI-generated content becomes ubiquitous.
