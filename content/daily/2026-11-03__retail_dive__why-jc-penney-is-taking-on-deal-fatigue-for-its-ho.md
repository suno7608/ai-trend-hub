<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

J.C. Penney, under parent company Catalyst Brands, is launching a new holiday campaign in partnership with agency Mischief. Led by Chief Customer and Marketing Officer Marisa Thalberg, the campaign directly addresses the phenomenon of 'deal fatigue' among consumers overwhelmed by promotional offers. The strategy signals a shift away from pure discount competition toward more differentiated customer engagement.
