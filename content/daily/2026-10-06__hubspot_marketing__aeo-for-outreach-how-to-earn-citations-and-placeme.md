기존 링크빌딩과 콘텐츠 아웃리치는 도메인 권위, 앵커 텍스트, 참조 도메인 등 검색엔진 중심의 신호에 최적화되어 있었으나, AI 답변 엔진은 이러한 신호를 다르게 해석한다. AI 가시성을 높이기 위해서는 검색엔진 순위 향상이 아닌 AI 답변에서의 인용(citation)과 배치(placement)를 목표로 한 새로운 아웃리치 전략이 필요하다. AEO 기반 아웃리치는 AI 시스템이 신뢰할 수 있는 정보 출처로 브랜드를 인식하게 만드는 데 초점을 맞춘다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Traditional link-building and content outreach strategies were designed around search engine signals such as domain authority, anchor text, and referring domains, but AI answer engines evaluate and surface content differently. Earning visibility in AI-generated answers requires a new outreach approach focused on securing citations and placements rather than simply improving search rankings. AEO-driven outreach aims to position a brand as a trusted, citable source that AI systems reliably reference in their responses.

</div>

## Key Points

- Traditional SEO outreach signals (domain authority, anchor text) do not translate directly to AI answer engine visibility
- AEO outreach focuses on earning citations and placements within AI-generated responses
- Brands must be recognized as trustworthy, citable sources by AI systems to gain answer engine presence
- A new outreach playbook is needed that is purpose-built for AI answer engine optimization
