Walmart이 머천트 및 공급업체를 위한 퍼스트파티 데이터 플랫폼 Scintilla에 마켓플레이스 데이터와 심화된 AI 기능을 추가한다고 발표했다. 이번 업그레이드는 Walmart Data Ventures의 Inspire 컨퍼런스에서 공개됐으며, 내년 중 출시될 예정이다. 이는 Walmart이 리테일 미디어 및 데이터 비즈니스를 강화해 공급업체와의 관계를 데이터 기반으로 심화시키려는 전략의 일환이다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Walmart announced a significant upgrade to Scintilla, its first-party data platform for merchants and suppliers, adding marketplace data and deeper AI features. The announcement was made at Walmart Data Ventures' Inspire conference, with the enhancements expected to roll out next year. This upgrade reflects Walmart's broader strategy to deepen data-driven relationships with suppliers while strengthening its retail media business.

</div>

## Key Points

- Walmart's Scintilla platform will add marketplace data and enhanced AI features for merchants and suppliers
- The upgrade was announced at the Walmart Data Ventures Inspire conference with a planned rollout next year
- The enhancement deepens Walmart's retail media capabilities and strengthens data ties with suppliers
- This mirrors a broader industry trend of major retailers monetizing first-party data through AI-enhanced insights platforms
