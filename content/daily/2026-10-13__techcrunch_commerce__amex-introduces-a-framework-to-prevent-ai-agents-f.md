아메리칸 익스프레스(Amex)가 AI 에이전트가 사용자의 명시적 승인 없이 구매를 실행하지 못하도록 방지하는 새로운 프레임워크를 도입했다. 이 프레임워크는 AI 기반 쇼핑이 성장함에 따라 발생할 수 있는 비용 오류와 분쟁을 사전에 방지하기 위해 설계되었다. AI 에이전트의 자율 구매 행동에 대한 신뢰와 안전을 확보하는 것이 에이전틱 커머스 성장의 핵심 과제임을 보여주는 사례다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

American Express has introduced a new framework designed to ensure AI agents only execute purchases that have been explicitly authorized by users, addressing growing concerns around AI-powered shopping errors and disputes. As AI agents increasingly act on behalf of consumers in commerce, the risk of unauthorized or mistaken transactions is becoming a critical issue for the financial services industry. The Amex framework represents an early industry effort to establish guardrails for agentic commerce at the payment infrastructure level.

</div>

## Key Points

- American Express is launching a framework to ensure AI agents only make user-authorized purchases
- The initiative directly addresses unauthorized transaction risks as AI-powered shopping scales
- This signals that financial infrastructure is beginning to build safety rails for agentic commerce
- Trust and authorization mechanisms will be critical for widespread adoption of AI shopping agents
