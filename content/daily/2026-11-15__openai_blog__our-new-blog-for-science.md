OpenAI has launched a dedicated science blog to share new research findings, practical methodologies, and firsthand accounts from researchers using AI for scientific discovery. The blog aims to serve as a communication channel between OpenAI's research teams and the broader scientific community. This initiative reflects OpenAI's strategy to build credibility and influence within academic and research circles.
