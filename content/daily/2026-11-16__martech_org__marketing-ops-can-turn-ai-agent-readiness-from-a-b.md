<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Disconnected data systems cause AI agents to make promises that brands cannot fulfill, representing a critical risk as agentic AI becomes more prevalent in customer interactions. Marketing operations teams have a unique opportunity to connect fragmented customer journey data and thereby transform AI agent readiness from a vulnerability into a growth driver. By taking ownership of AI agent infrastructure, marketing ops can play a central role in delivering meaningful business growth.
