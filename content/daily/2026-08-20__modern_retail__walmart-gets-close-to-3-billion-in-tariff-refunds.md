<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Walmart has received close to $3 billion in tariff refunds, significantly bolstering its balance sheet amid ongoing trade policy fluctuations. While large retailers benefit from these refunds, consumers continue to face spending constraints driven by elevated fuel prices and broader macroeconomic pressures. This dynamic creates an uneven competitive landscape between major retail chains and smaller D2C operators who lack the scale to absorb tariff impacts.
