</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Snapchat has integrated with HubSpot to enhance its lead generation ad capabilities, allowing advertisers to manage Snapchat leads directly within the HubSpot CRM environment. The move is part of Snapchat's broader strategy of building partnerships to reduce friction for advertisers and agencies. VP of Global Agency and Strategic Accounts Adrian Mulryan emphasized meeting clients within their existing workflows.
