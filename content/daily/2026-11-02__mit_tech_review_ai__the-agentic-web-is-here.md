<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

The 'agentic web' — where autonomous AI agents navigate the internet on behalf of users — has arrived, echoing Tim Berners-Lee's 2001 vision of a Semantic Web designed for software agents. These agents go beyond simple information retrieval, performing complex multi-step tasks autonomously and fundamentally reshaping how the web is experienced. This marks a paradigm shift across search, shopping, and content consumption with broad implications for every digital industry.

</div>

## Key Points

- The 'agentic web' marks the realization of Tim Berners-Lee's 2001 Semantic Web vision, with AI agents autonomously navigating the internet
- AI agents perform complex, multi-step tasks beyond simple search, fundamentally changing digital interaction patterns
- This shift has sweeping implications for e-commerce, content discovery, and brand visibility in D2C channels
- Businesses that fail to optimize for agentic web interactions risk becoming invisible to AI-mediated consumers
