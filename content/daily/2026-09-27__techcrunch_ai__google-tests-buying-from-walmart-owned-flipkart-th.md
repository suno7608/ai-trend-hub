Google is testing a direct purchasing feature in India allowing users to buy products from Walmart-owned Flipkart through Gemini and AI Mode. The trial is currently limited to select products and users, with a broader rollout planned for October. This represents a concrete implementation of agentic commerce where AI assistants bridge search and transaction.
