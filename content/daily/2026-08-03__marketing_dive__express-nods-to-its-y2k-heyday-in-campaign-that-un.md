<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Fashion retailer Express launched a fall campaign channeling its Y2K heyday by featuring pop star Tinashe and NFL player Tee Higgins. CMO Joe Berean described the effort as an iteration of the brand's 'Dress to Express' platform, blending fashion and music culture. The campaign targets millennial and Gen Z consumers through culturally resonant storytelling.
