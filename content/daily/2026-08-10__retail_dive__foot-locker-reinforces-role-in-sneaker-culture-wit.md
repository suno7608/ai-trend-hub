<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Foot Locker has launched a new brand platform titled 'It Always Will Be Foot Locker,' aimed at reinforcing its identity within sneaker culture. Chief Marketing Officer Brett O'Brien spearheaded the creative initiative, which coincides with the retailer's broader business turnaround efforts. The campaign focuses on cultural community connection as the key differentiator in an increasingly competitive sneaker market.
