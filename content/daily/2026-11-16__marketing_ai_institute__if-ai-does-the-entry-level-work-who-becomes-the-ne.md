<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

SmarterX founder and CEO Paul Roetzer opened MAICON 2026 with the candid admission that had ChatGPT existed in 2000, he likely would never have broken into marketing. This raises a fundamental question about the future of talent development: if AI handles entry-level marketing work, who trains and nurtures the next generation of leaders? The session highlights the urgent need for organizations to rethink their talent pipelines and capability development strategies in an AI-accelerated environment.
