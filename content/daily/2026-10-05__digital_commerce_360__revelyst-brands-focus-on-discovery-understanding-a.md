Revelyst, which manages over a dozen outdoor recreation brands, has observed a significant rise in AI-driven product discovery in 2026 and is actively working to understand what consumers are learning about its products through AI platforms. The company notes that consumers are increasingly discovering its products outside of its own brand websites. This trend underscores the growing importance of managing AI-generated traffic as a distinct strategic channel.
