<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

A MarTech.org piece draws on the history of WYSIWYG editors to argue that technology consistently shifts where human skills matter rather than eliminating them entirely. The rise of AI tools, including 'vibe coding,' follows this same pattern by redefining the roles of marketers and technologists. The core argument is that human judgment and creativity remain essential but must adapt to new technological contexts.

</div>

## Key Points

- Historical tech shifts like WYSIWYG editors relocated rather than eliminated human skills
- AI tools including vibe coding follow the same pattern of skill displacement, not elimination
- Organizations must proactively redesign roles to capture human value in AI-augmented workflows
