OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has publicly called on the AI industry to slow the pace of AI development, reigniting the broader 'decel' debate. The discussion was featured on TechCrunch's Equity podcast, highlighting growing tensions between AI acceleration and caution within the tech community. Altman's stance signals a potential shift in how leading AI companies think about responsible deployment timelines.
