<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Tubby Todd's co-founder discussed on the Modern Retail Podcast how private equity investment enabled the baby care brand's transition from a direct-to-consumer model to securing shelf space at Target. PE funding provides growth capital but can come with trade-offs around brand control and strategic autonomy. The conversation highlights a broader trend of DTC brands leveraging external capital to expand into traditional retail channels.
