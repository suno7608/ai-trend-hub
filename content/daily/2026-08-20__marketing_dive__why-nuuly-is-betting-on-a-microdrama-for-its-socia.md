<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Nuuly, the high-growth clothing rental service owned by Urban Outfitters, is launching a social-first fall campaign built around a microdrama series exploring modern dating themes. The brand is using narrative-driven content to overcome ad fatigue rather than relying on traditional advertising formats. The strategy aims to drive organic reach and brand awareness through compelling storytelling on social media platforms.
