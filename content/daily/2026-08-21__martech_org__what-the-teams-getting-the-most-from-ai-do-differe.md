<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Marketing teams that extract the most value from AI share three organizational characteristics: autonomy, a culture of experimentation, and cross-functional collaboration. These structural traits allow teams to harness AI's speed advantage to ship campaigns faster without increasing headcount. The article emphasizes that organizational design is as critical as the AI tools themselves in driving performance.

</div>

## Key Points

- Autonomy, experimentation, and cross-functional work are the defining traits of high-performing AI teams
- AI enables faster campaign execution without headcount growth when paired with the right org structure
- Organizational design is a critical enabler—not just the AI technology itself
- Teams lacking these cultural traits risk underutilizing AI investments
