<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Airbnb has announced plans to launch an AI travel agent in the first half of 2027, designed to assist travelers throughout their entire journey. The AI agent will help users plan trips, compare options, manage bookings, and provide ongoing assistance during their stay. This move represents a significant step toward agentic commerce, where AI autonomously handles complex, multi-step consumer transactions.
