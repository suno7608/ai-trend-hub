<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

The Seattle Times and Newsday have joined a growing list of news organizations filing lawsuits against OpenAI and Microsoft, alleging their journalism was used without permission to train AI models. This legal action reflects an escalating industry-wide dispute over intellectual property rights in the context of AI training data. The outcome of these cases could set significant legal precedents for how AI companies source and use third-party content.
