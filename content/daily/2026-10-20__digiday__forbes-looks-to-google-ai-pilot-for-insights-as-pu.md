<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Forbes has joined Google's paid News AI pilot, granting Google expanded rights to use its content to test AI-driven contextual reader experiences before a click occurs. The initiative represents publishers' efforts to discover value beyond traditional click-through metrics in an AI-mediated content landscape. This signals a broader industry shift in how publishers and platforms negotiate content value in the age of AI search.

## Key Points

- Forbes participates in Google's paid News AI pilot, expanding content licensing to Google
- Pilot tests AI-generated context delivery to readers before they click on articles
- Publishers are actively seeking monetization models beyond click-based traffic
- AI search intermediation is reshaping the publisher-platform content value exchange
