OpenAI has reversed its earlier opposition to California's AI safety bill SB 53, now actively calling for the legislation to be strengthened. The shift highlights the rapidly evolving regulatory landscape around AI and the growing role of major AI companies in shaping policy. Tightening AI safety regulations could become a significant policy variable affecting the adoption of AI-driven commerce and marketing solutions.
