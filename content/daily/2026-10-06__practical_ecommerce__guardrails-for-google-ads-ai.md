<div class="lang-ko">

Google Ads의 AI 자동화가 고도화되면서, 광고주가 AI에게 무엇을 제외하도록 설정하는지가 타겟팅 설정만큼 중요해지고 있다. AI는 효율적으로 작동하지만, 브랜드 안전성이나 비즈니스 목표에 맞지 않는 방향으로 예산을 집행할 위험이 있다. 따라서 명확한 가드레일 설정이 AI 기반 광고 캠페인의 성과와 브랜드 보호를 위한 핵심 과제로 떠오르고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

As Google Ads AI automation becomes more sophisticated, what advertisers instruct the AI to exclude is proving just as critical as what they target. Without proper guardrails, AI systems risk misallocating budgets in ways that conflict with brand safety or business objectives. Establishing clear exclusion parameters is emerging as a foundational practice for effective and brand-safe AI-driven advertising.

</div>

## Key Points

- Exclusion settings in Google Ads AI are as strategically important as targeting configurations
- AI automation without guardrails can lead to brand safety risks and budget misallocation
- Advertisers must actively define what AI should avoid, not just what it should pursue
- Effective guardrail frameworks are becoming a core competency for AI-driven ad management
