메타(Meta)는 자사 AI 어시스턴트 'Muse'가 경쟁사 'OpenClaw'로부터 '강한 영감을 받았음'을 인정했다. 메타는 Muse가 자체적으로 개발되었다고 주장하지만, 워크스페이스 파일명과 일부 콘텐츠까지 유사하다는 점을 시인했다. 이 사건은 AI 업계에서 지식재산권 및 모델 독창성 논란을 다시 수면 위로 올리고 있다.

<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Meta has acknowledged that its AI assistant Muse was 'heavily inspired' by rival product OpenClaw, despite claiming it was built from scratch. Meta admitted the similarity extends to some workspace filenames and content within the systems. The controversy reignites ongoing debates around intellectual property and originality in AI development.

</div>

## Key Points

- Meta admits Muse was 'heavily inspired' by OpenClaw, including shared workspace filenames
- Meta maintains Muse was independently built from scratch, creating a contradictory narrative
- The case highlights growing IP and originality risks in competitive AI development
- This controversy has broader implications for trust and transparency in AI product launches
