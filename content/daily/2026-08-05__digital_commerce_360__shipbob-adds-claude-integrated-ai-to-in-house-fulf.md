ShipBob, the global supply chain and fulfillment platform, has launched an AI suite that connects to merchants' live operations via either its proprietary agent 'Bobby' or Anthropic's Claude. The company built its fulfillment technology stack entirely in-house and is now layering end-to-end AI capabilities across its entire operations. This integration aims to improve logistics efficiency and real-time decision-making for D2C merchants.
