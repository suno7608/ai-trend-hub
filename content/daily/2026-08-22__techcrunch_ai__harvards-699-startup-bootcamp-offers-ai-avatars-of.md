Harvard Business School has launched HBS Foundry, a $699 startup bootcamp that features AI avatars modeled after its instructors. These AI avatars provide real-time feedback during practice pitch sessions and simulated board meetings. The initiative signals a growing commercialization of educational AI and the digitization of expert personas in higher education.
