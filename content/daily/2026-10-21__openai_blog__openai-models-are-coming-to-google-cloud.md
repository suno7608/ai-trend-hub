<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

OpenAI and Google Cloud have announced a partnership to bring OpenAI's frontier models and Codex workflows to Gemini's Model Garden, accessible via the Google Cloud Marketplace. This collaboration allows developers to access OpenAI models directly within the Google Cloud ecosystem. The partnership represents a notable cross-industry collaboration between two major AI players.
