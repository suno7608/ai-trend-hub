D2C fashion brand Quince has unveiled its largest-ever campaign featuring Friends star David Schwimmer in a holiday-themed narrative about searching for the perfect red sweater. The campaign marks a significant scale-up in Quince's marketing investment, using celebrity storytelling to elevate brand awareness during the peak holiday season. The campaign relies on traditional celebrity-driven creative rather than AI-powered marketing approaches.
