At Advertising Week, Free People CMO Jack Reynolds explained the brand's strategy of 'cultural co-authorship' in celebrity partnerships, exemplified by their collaboration with musician Willow. The Urban Outfitters-owned retailer deliberately cedes creative control to celebrity partners rather than dictating brand messaging. This approach prioritizes authentic cultural connection over traditional brand-controlled marketing narratives.
