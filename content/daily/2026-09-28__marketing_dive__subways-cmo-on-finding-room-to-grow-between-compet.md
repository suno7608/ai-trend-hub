<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Subway CMO Jeff Klein unveiled a new brand platform featuring actor Will Arnett, aiming to find growth space between intensifying competitors in the QSR sector. The campaign represents a strategic effort to reestablish differentiated positioning in a saturated fast-food market.
