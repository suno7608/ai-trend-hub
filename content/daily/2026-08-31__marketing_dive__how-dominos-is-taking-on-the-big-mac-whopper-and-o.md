<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Domino's CMO Kate Trumbull, working with agency of record Work In Progress, launched a bold campaign directly challenging fast-food icons like McDonald's Big Mac and Burger King's Whopper. The campaign is characterized by a hyperbolic, darkly comedic tone designed to stand out in a crowded competitive landscape. The move signals Domino's ambition to compete beyond the pizza category and reposition itself within the broader fast-food market.
