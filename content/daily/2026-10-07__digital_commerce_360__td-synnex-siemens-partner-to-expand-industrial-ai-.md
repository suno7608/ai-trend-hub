TD Synnex and Siemens have announced a global partnership to distribute industrial AI and IT-OT (Information Technology-Operational Technology) integrated solutions. The collaboration combines TD Synnex's IT offerings with Siemens' infrastructure and automation portfolio to address growing demand for AI in industrial use cases. The partnership targets both software and physical AI, aiming to accelerate digital transformation in manufacturing and industrial sectors.
