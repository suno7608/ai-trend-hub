<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Adobe has been named the Official Creative, Marketing, and AI Partner of the NHL, signaling a major integration of AI into professional sports marketing. NHL teams will gain access to Adobe's AI-powered creative and marketing solutions as part of the partnership. This collaboration highlights the growing role of AI-driven platforms in entertainment and sports marketing ecosystems.
