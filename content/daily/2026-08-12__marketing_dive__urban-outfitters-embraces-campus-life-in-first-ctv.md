<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Urban Outfitters has launched its first-ever connected TV (CTV) commercial as part of the 'Back-To-Campus' brand platform, signaling a strategic push to reach Gen Z audiences. The high-energy spot capitalizes on campus culture themes as the retailer continues to prioritize Gen Z engagement. The CTV investment reflects a broader media mix diversification trend among retail brands targeting younger, streaming-native consumers.
