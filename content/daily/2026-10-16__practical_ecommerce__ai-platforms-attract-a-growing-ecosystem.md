<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Apps, connectors, marketplaces, protocols, and payment systems are beginning to coalesce around AI platforms, forming new commercial ecosystems. This development indicates that AI is evolving beyond a standalone tool into a foundational platform layer. Ecommerce businesses that integrate early into these ecosystems may gain significant competitive advantages.

## Key Points

- AI platforms are attracting surrounding ecosystems including apps, marketplaces, and payment systems

- Early ecosystem participation could provide significant competitive advantages for D2C brands
- New protocols and connectors are emerging to enable AI-native commerce workflows
