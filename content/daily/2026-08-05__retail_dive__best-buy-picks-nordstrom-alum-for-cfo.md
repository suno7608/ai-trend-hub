<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Best Buy has appointed Anne Bramman, a veteran from Nordstrom, as its new Chief Financial Officer, succeeding Matt Bilunas who departed last month after seven years in the role. Bramman brings premium retail finance experience to Best Buy as the company continues its AI and digital transformation investments. The leadership change occurs at a critical period for the consumer electronics retailer.
