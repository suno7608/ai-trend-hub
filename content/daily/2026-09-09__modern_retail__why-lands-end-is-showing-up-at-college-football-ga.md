<div class="lang-ko">

랜즈 엔드(Lands' End)는 브랜드 인지도 확대를 위해 올 가을 대학 미식축구 게임데이를 중심으로 집중 마케팅 캠페인을 전개한다. 6개월 전 합류한 최고마케팅책임자(CMO) 사라 실베스터가 이 전략을 주도하고 있으며, 게임데이를 랜즈 엔드가 선점할 수 있는 문화적 순간으로 보고 있다. 이 캠페인은 신규 고객층에게 브랜드를 소개하는 것을 핵심 목표로 삼고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Lands' End is launching a focused marketing push around college football game days this fall to expand brand awareness among new audiences. The strategy is led by CMO Sarah Sylvester, who joined the company approximately six months ago and sees game day culture as a key moment the brand can own. The initiative represents a broader effort to introduce Lands' End to consumers who may not yet be familiar with the brand.

</div>

## Key Points

- Lands' End is targeting college football game days as a brand-building platform this fall
- CMO Sarah Sylvester, hired ~6 months ago, is driving the strategy with a focus on new audience acquisition
- The campaign is positioned as a cultural moment Lands' End can 'own' rather than a generic sponsorship
- Core objective is brand introduction to consumers unfamiliar with Lands' End
