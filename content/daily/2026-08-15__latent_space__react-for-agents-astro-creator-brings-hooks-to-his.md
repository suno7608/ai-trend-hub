Astro 창시자 Fred Schott가 AI 에이전트 프레임워크 Flue의 두 번째 버전을 공개하며 React의 훅(Hooks) 개념을 도입했다. Flue 2는 에이전트의 동작 방식이 그것을 감싸는 '하네스(harness)'에 의해 정의된다는 철학을 기반으로 설계되었다. 이 접근 방식은 프론트엔드 개발자에게 친숙한 패턴을 에이전트 개발에 적용해 진입 장벽을 낮추려는 시도다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Flue 2, created by Fred Schott—known for building the Astro web framework—draws direct inspiration from React by introducing hooks into AI agent development. The framework is built on the philosophy that agents are fundamentally defined by their harnesses, the meta-structures that wrap and control their behavior. This approach aims to lower the barrier to agent development by applying patterns familiar to frontend engineers.

</div>

## Key Points

- Flue 2 introduces React-inspired hooks into AI agent development workflows
- Creator Fred Schott (Astro) argues that agents are defined by their harnesses, not just their models
- The framework lowers the technical barrier for frontend developers entering agent engineering
