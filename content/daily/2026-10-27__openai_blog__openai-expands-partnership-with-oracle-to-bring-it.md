</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

This collaboration marks a significant step in transforming ChatGPT from a conversational AI tool into a comprehensive enterprise business platform. Enterprise users will be able to access and operate Oracle business applications seamlessly within the ChatGPT interface.
