<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Caesars Sportsbook launched a campaign featuring former Green Bay Packers star Clay Matthews undergoing a 'looksmaxxing' makeover, tapping into a viral Gen Z cultural trend as part of its 'We've Had Some Work Done' sportsbook app campaign. Matthews' new look went viral on social media, generating significant organic attention for the brand. The campaign exemplifies how sports betting brands are blending pop culture trends with celebrity athlete endorsements to drive awareness.
