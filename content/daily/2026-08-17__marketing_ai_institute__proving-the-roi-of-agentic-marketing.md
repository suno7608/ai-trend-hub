</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

The Marketing AI Institute argues that the true value of AI in marketing goes beyond time savings, emphasizing that the real goal is reallocating saved time toward higher-value activities. Proving the ROI of agentic marketing requires connecting AI-driven efficiencies directly to measurable business outcomes, not just productivity metrics. This perspective was a central theme at MAICON 2026, offering strategic guidance for organizations looking to justify and scale AI marketing investments.
