AI-powered shopping tools are emerging as a potential solution to high e-commerce return rates by equipping consumers with better product information before purchase. Better-informed shoppers are more likely to buy items they will ultimately keep, reducing the costly reverse logistics burden on retailers. This development presents a dual opportunity for D2C brands to cut operational costs while improving customer satisfaction.
