David's Bridal announced plans to spin off its AI platform Pearl into a stand-alone, publicly traded company. Pearl will own the wedding retailer's intellectual property and brand assets, with current CEO Kelly Cook expected to lead the new entity. This move reflects a growing trend of retailers separating AI-driven assets into independent business units to unlock distinct market value.
