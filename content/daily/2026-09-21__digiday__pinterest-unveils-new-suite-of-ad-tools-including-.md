<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Pinterest unveiled a new suite of advertising tools, headlined by Visual Search Ads, as part of its strategic pitch to attract more brand advertisers. The platform highlighted its growing and increasingly younger user base as a key differentiator for companies seeking to reach next-generation consumers. Visual Search Ads aim to convert image-based discovery behavior directly into purchase intent, presenting a compelling opportunity for D2C brands.
