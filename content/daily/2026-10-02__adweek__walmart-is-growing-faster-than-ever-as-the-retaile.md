Walmart+ is growing at its fastest pace ever while Walmart has maintained the annual membership price at $98 since its 2020 launch. The retailer has been consistently adding more perks to increase the program's perceived value. This demonstrates the effectiveness of a membership growth strategy based on benefit expansion rather than price increases.
