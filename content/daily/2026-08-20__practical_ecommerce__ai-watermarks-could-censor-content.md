<div class="lang-ko">

Anthropic이 EU AI법(AI Act) 준수를 위해 AI 생성 텍스트에 워터마크를 삽입하는 기술을 도입했다. 이 워터마크는 머신 생성 콘텐츠를 식별 가능하게 만들기 위한 규제 요건에서 비롯되었다. 그러나 일부에서는 이 기술이 AI 생성 콘텐츠를 사실상 검열하는 수단으로 작용할 수 있다는 우려를 제기하고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Anthropic has introduced a text-based watermarking system for AI-generated content in response to the EU's AI Act, which mandates that machine-generated content be detectable. The watermark embeds identifiable signals into AI-produced text to ensure regulatory compliance. However, concerns are emerging that such watermarking mechanisms could effectively function as a form of content censorship.

</div>

## Key Points

- Anthropic introduced text-based AI watermarking technology driven by EU AI Act requirements
- The regulation mandates that AI-generated content must be identifiable/detectable
- Watermarks raise concerns about potential censorship effects on AI-generated marketing content
- Compliance implications extend to any brand using AI content tools in the EU
