<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

1-800-Flowers.com has completed the sale of its PersonalizationMall.com and Things Remembered business units to PlanetArt, a technology platform company. The transaction closed in under a month after the final deal announcement, as 1-800-Flowers seeks to concentrate on its core business priorities amid a challenging year. The divestiture highlights how gifting and personalization ecommerce players are restructuring amid market pressures.
