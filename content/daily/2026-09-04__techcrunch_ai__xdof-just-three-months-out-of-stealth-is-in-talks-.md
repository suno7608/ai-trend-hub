Robot data startup XDOF is in talks to raise a Series B round at a $1.2 billion valuation, just three months after emerging from stealth. The company focuses on providing data for robot training, tapping into surging investor interest in AI robotics infrastructure. The rapid trajectory from stealth to unicorn-level valuation reflects the intense capital momentum behind AI robotics data platforms.
