<div class="lang-ko">

HubSpot은 브랜드가 AI 답변 엔진(AEO)에서 소외되는 콘텐츠 격차를 진단하고 수정하는 감사 방법을 안내한다. AEO 격차는 AI 답변 엔진이 특정 페이지를 출처로 활용하지 못하거나 않으려는 모든 원인을 의미한다. 브랜드들이 SEO를 넘어 AI 검색 시대에 맞는 콘텐츠 전략으로 전환해야 할 필요성이 강조된다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

HubSpot outlines a structured content audit approach for diagnosing and fixing Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) gaps that prevent brands from appearing in AI-generated answers. An AEO gap is defined as any reason an AI answer engine cannot or will not use a brand's page as a source. The guide underscores the growing urgency for brands to adapt their content strategy beyond traditional SEO to remain visible in AI-driven search environments.

</div>

## Key Points

- AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is defined as ensuring AI answer engines can use brand pages as sources
- A structured content audit is the recommended first step to identify and fix AEO gaps
- Brands must evolve content strategies beyond traditional SEO to maintain visibility in AI-powered search
