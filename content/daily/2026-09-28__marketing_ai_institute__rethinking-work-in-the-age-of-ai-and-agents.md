Paul Roetzer, Founder and CEO of SmarterX and Marketing AI Institute, will deliver the opening keynote at MAICON 2026 titled 'The Architect, The Orchestrator and The Apprentice: Rethinking Work in the Age of AI and Agents.' The keynote introduces a framework of three evolving human roles in an AI-augmented workplace. The session is designed to help marketing and business leaders rethink how work is structured as AI agents become integral to operations.

- MAICON 2026 keynote introduces a three-role framework: Architect, Orchestrator, and Apprentice for human-AI collaboration

- The session addresses how AI agents are fundamentally reshaping work structures and organizational roles
