<div class="lang-ko">

Flo와 Motorway는 LLM(대형 언어 모델)에서 자사 콘텐츠가 인용되도록 하기 위한 전용 AI 검색 전략을 개발하고 있다. Flo는 AI 검색 노출을 담당하는 전담 'AI 디스커버리' 역할을 신설했으며, Motorway는 콘텐츠가 LLM에서 24시간 이내에 인용된다고 밝혔다. 이는 AI 검색이 기존 SEO를 대체하거나 보완하는 새로운 마케팅 전쟁터로 부상하고 있음을 보여준다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Flo and Motorway shared their strategies for getting brand content cited by AI language models, at the Semrush Spotlight event. Flo has created a dedicated 'AI discovery' role to manage and optimize visibility within LLM outputs, while Motorway claims its content is being cited by LLMs within 24 hours of publication. These approaches signal the emergence of a new discipline in content marketing focused on AI citation and discoverability.

</div>

## Key Points

- Flo has created a dedicated 'AI discovery' role to optimize brand visibility in LLM outputs
- Motorway reports its content is being cited by LLMs within 24 hours of publication
- AI citation optimization is emerging as a distinct and critical new discipline within content marketing
- Traditional SEO strategies alone are insufficient as AI-driven search becomes dominant
