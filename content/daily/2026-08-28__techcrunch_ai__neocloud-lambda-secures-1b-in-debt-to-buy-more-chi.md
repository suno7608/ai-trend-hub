<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Neocloud company Lambda has secured $1 billion in private debt to purchase Nvidia AI chips and lease them to Microsoft, representing the latest in a series of large-scale AI infrastructure investments. This move underscores the enormous capital requirements of sustaining the ongoing AI boom. The deal highlights the growing role of neocloud intermediaries in bridging chip supply to major tech players.
