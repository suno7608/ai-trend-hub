<div class="lang-ko">

재고 왜곡(inventory distortion)은 전 세계 리테일 업계에 연간 1조 7,700억 달러의 손실을 초래하고 있으며, 단순한 재고 카운팅 증가로는 해결할 수 없다. 근본적인 해법은 더 정확하고 통합된 데이터 인프라를 구축하는 것이다. 데이터 품질 개선 없이는 AI 기반 수요 예측이나 재고 최적화도 효과를 발휘하기 어렵다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Inventory distortion costs the retail industry $1.77 trillion annually, a problem that cannot be solved simply by counting inventory more frequently. The real fix lies in better, more integrated data infrastructure that enables accurate demand forecasting and stock management. Without improved data quality, even advanced AI-driven inventory solutions will fall short of their potential.

</div>

## Key Points

- Inventory distortion results in $1.77 trillion in annual losses for global retail
- Simply increasing physical inventory counts does not solve the root problem
- Better data quality and integration are the foundational fix for inventory distortion
- AI-powered solutions depend on high-quality data to deliver meaningful improvements
