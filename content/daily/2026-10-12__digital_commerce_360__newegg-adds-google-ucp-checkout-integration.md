<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Consumer electronics retailer Newegg has integrated Google's Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) for online checkout, formally expanding its presence in agentic commerce channels. Newegg joins major retailers including Wayfair, Walmart, and Macy's that have supported UCP since Google officially launched it in January. The growing adoption of UCP signals accelerating standardization of AI agent-driven shopping infrastructure across retail.

- Consumer electronics is now a key vertical for agentic commerce channel expansion
