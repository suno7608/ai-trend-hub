Sephora has cast actor Seth Rogen as a clueless shopper in its holiday ad campaign, targeting gift-givers who are unfamiliar with the beauty category. The campaign uses humor to resonate with consumers who want to give thoughtful presents but lack beauty product knowledge. This strategy allows Sephora to expand its reach beyond core beauty enthusiasts to a broader holiday gifting audience.
