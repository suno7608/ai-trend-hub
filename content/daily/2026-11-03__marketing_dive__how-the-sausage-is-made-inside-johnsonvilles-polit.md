<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Johnsonville launched a political parody campaign timed to the U.S. midterm elections, designed to engage Americans around shared frustration with politics without expressing partisan views. The campaign leveraged humor and cultural relevance to build brand affinity among a broad consumer base. The campaign culminated on Election Day to maximize timely marketing impact.
