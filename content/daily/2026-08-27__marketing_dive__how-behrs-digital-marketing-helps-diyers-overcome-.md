<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Behr CMO Andy Lopez revealed how the paint brand is using an AI-powered hue-choosing chatbot combined with an influencer campaign as part of a renewed digital transformation strategy. The initiative specifically targets the consumer pain point of 'paint paralysis'—the overwhelming challenge of selecting the right color. By combining AI personalization tools with creator-driven content, Behr is aiming to convert digital engagement into purchase decisions.

## Key Points

- Behr deployed an AI chatbot specifically designed to assist with color/product selection decisions
- The chatbot addresses 'paint paralysis'—a consumer friction point that mirrors decision fatigue in complex product categories
- Influencer marketing is integrated alongside AI tools to amplify reach and trust
- This reflects a broader trend of brands using conversational AI to convert digital engagement into purchase action
