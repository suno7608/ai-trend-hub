<div class="lang-ko">

DoorDash가 Instacart를 170억 달러에 인수할 계획을 발표하며 배달 플랫폼 간 시장 점유율 경쟁이 새로운 국면에 접어들었다. 이번 인수는 단순 음식 배달을 넘어 식료품, 광고, 리테일 미디어 등 서비스 다각화를 통해 플랫폼 가치를 극대화하려는 전략으로 풀이된다. 현대 배달 플랫폼이 광고 수익 모델과 소매 데이터를 결합한 슈퍼 플랫폼으로 진화하고 있음을 보여준다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

DoorDash has announced plans to acquire Instacart for $17 billion, underscoring the need for delivery platforms to diversify their services to capture greater market share. The acquisition reflects a broader strategy to combine food delivery, grocery, retail media, and advertising into a unified commerce ecosystem. This move signals the evolution of delivery platforms into multi-vertical super-platforms with significant data and advertising capabilities.

</div>

## Key Points

- DoorDash plans to acquire Instacart for $17 billion, marking a major consolidation in the delivery space
- Platform diversification—spanning food, grocery, and retail media—is now a core competitive requirement
- Combined platforms gain significant first-party data and advertising inventory advantages
- The deal reflects the ongoing convergence of commerce, logistics, and digital advertising
