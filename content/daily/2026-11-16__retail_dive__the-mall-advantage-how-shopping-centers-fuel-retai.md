</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Physical shopping centers are proving their value beyond point-of-sale transactions by driving brand discovery, stimulating digital demand, and improving operational efficiencies. Malls serve as powerful brand awareness engines where in-store experiences convert into online purchase intent. The complementary relationship between physical and digital retail channels is emerging as a key driver of overall retail growth.

- 오프라인 경험이 온라인 구매 전환을 촉진하는 옴니채널 선순환 구조 형성
- 운영 효율성 측면에서도 물리적 쇼핑센터가 중요한 역할을 수행함
