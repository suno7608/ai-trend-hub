<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Google has outlined ambitious plans to expand its digital wallet's role in commerce well beyond point-of-sale payments. The company's payments head described a vision where Google Wallet becomes a central infrastructure layer in agentic retail, where AI agents act on behalf of consumers. This positions Google to capture a critical role in the emerging autonomous commerce ecosystem.

## Key Points

- Google plans to expand its digital wallet far beyond point-of-sale payment functionality
- The vision includes Google Wallet as a core layer in agentic retail, where AI agents transact on behalf of users
- Google's payments head publicly articulated this broader commerce ambition
- This signals Google's intent to compete as a full-stack commerce platform, not just a payments tool
