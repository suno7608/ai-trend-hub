Nordstrom Rack is returning to San Francisco after a three-year absence, with plans to open a 51,500-square-foot store in fall 2027. The retailer previously closed its Union Square location but is now re-entering the market as part of the city's ongoing retail revitalization. The move signals renewed retailer confidence in the San Francisco urban retail environment.
