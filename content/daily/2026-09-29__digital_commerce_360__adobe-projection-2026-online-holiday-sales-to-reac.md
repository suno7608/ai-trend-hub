Adobe has projected that U.S. online holiday sales will reach $275.1 billion in the final two months of 2026. The forecast is based on analysis of more than 1 trillion visits to U.S. retail sites, 100 million SKUs, and 18 product categories. The projection underscores the continued and substantial growth of e-commerce during the holiday season.
