<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

OpenAI has decided to wind down its contract providing AI models to Cursor following the coding tool's acquisition by SpaceX. The decision reflects potential competitive or conflict-of-interest concerns arising from the ownership change. This case illustrates how major corporate acquisitions in the AI ecosystem can disrupt established vendor-client relationships.

## Key Points

- OpenAI is terminating its model supply contract with Cursor after SpaceX acquired the coding platform
- The decision signals that OpenAI will reassess partnerships when clients are acquired by potentially competing entities
- This sets a precedent for how ownership changes can disrupt AI tool supply chains
