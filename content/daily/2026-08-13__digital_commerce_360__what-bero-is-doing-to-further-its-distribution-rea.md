<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Non-alcoholic beer brand Bero, which launched in late 2024, is rapidly expanding its U.S. distribution network to include major retailers such as Walmart, Kroger, Publix, Target, H.E.B., and Albertsons. The brand has developed an internal portal to streamline its B2B distribution processes, according to its VP of Sales Declan Duggan. Bero's growth reflects the broader expansion of the non-alcoholic beverage market and the value of a multi-channel distribution strategy combining D2C and retail.
