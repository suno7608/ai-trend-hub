</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

MIT Technology Review argues that debates over AI consciousness, rogue agents, and autonomous actors risk becoming a rhetorical trap that distracts from substantive AI policy discussions. Prominent tech leaders including Demis Hassabis, Dario Amodei, and Sam Altman advocate for regulation of seemingly superhuman AI systems, while separate policy factions offer contrasting views. The anthropomorphization of AI agents in public discourse may obscure more practical governance and operational challenges.

</div>

## Key Points

- Rhetoric around AI consciousness and 'rogue agents' may distort practical policy and governance discussions
- Key tech leaders (Hassabis, Amodei, Altman) are pushing for regulation of 'superhuman' AI systems
- Anthropomorphizing AI agents risks misallocating attention away from real operational risks
- The debate reflects a broader tension between AI safety advocates and policy pragmatists
