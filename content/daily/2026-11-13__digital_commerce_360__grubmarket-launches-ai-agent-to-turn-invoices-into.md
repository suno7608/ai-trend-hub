GrubMarket은 식품 유통업체를 위해 공급업체 인보이스와 영수증을 자동으로 구매 주문서로 변환하는 AI 에이전트 'AI Orders'를 출시했다. 캘리포니아 사우스샌프란시스코에 본사를 둔 이 SaaS 기업은 식품 생산자, 도매업체, 유통업체, 소매업체를 대상으로 서비스를 제공한다. 이번 AI 에이전트 도입은 B2B 식품 공급망의 행정 업무 자동화를 통해 운영 효율을 높이는 것을 목표로 한다.

<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

GrubMarket, a SaaS company serving food producers, wholesalers, distributors, and retailers, has launched an AI agent called 'AI Orders' that automatically converts vendor invoices and receipts into purchase orders. The tool is designed specifically to streamline procurement workflows for food distributors by eliminating manual data entry. This expansion into agentic AI reflects GrubMarket's broader strategy to automate B2B supply chain operations.

</div>

## Key Points

- GrubMarket launched 'AI Orders', an AI agent that converts vendor invoices and receipts into purchase orders automatically
- The tool targets food distributors to eliminate manual procurement data entry
- Represents a practical application of agentic AI in B2B supply chain operations
- GrubMarket serves food producers, wholesalers, distributors, and retailers as a SaaS platform
