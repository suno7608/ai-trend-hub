<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Asics America has deployed autonomous AI-powered drones developed by Corvus Robotics at its primary North American distribution center to locate misplaced inventory. The technology enables the company to recover misplaced merchandise approximately 60% faster, reducing the risk of unfulfilled orders. The deployment was officially announced on November 10, highlighting tangible results from AI-driven warehouse automation.
