Instacart가 북미 사용자를 위한 AI 어시스턴트 'Clementine'을 출시하여 식료품 쇼핑의 복잡한 의사결정을 지원하고, 기존 엔터프라이즈 AI 도구 Cart Assistant의 파트너십도 확대했다. Clementine은 무엇을 살지 결정하는 것과 같은 '식료품 쇼핑에서 가장 어려운 부분'을 해결하는 데 초점을 맞추고 있다. 이는 Instacart가 에이전틱 커머스 생태계를 본격적으로 구축하며 AI 기반 쇼핑 자동화를 강화하는 중요한 행보다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Instacart has launched Clementine, an AI assistant for North American users designed to handle the most challenging aspects of grocery shopping, including complex purchase decisions. The company simultaneously expanded partnerships for its enterprise AI tool, Cart Assistant, deepening its agentic commerce ecosystem. This move signals Instacart's strategic commitment to building a comprehensive AI-powered shopping automation infrastructure.

</div>

## Key Points

- Clementine targets the hardest parts of grocery shopping, including product selection decisions, signaling AI's role in complex purchase scenarios
- Instacart is simultaneously scaling both consumer-facing (Clementine) and enterprise-facing (Cart Assistant) AI tools, building a two-sided agentic commerce platform
- Expanded partner integrations for Cart Assistant indicate Instacart is positioning itself as an agentic commerce infrastructure provider
