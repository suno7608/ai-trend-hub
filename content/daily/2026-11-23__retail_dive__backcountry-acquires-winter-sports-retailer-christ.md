Outdoor retailer Backcountry has acquired winter sports retailer Christy Sports, creating a combined retail footprint of more than 60 locations. The deal expands parent company CSC Generation's brand portfolio and adds a large ski and snowboard rental network. This acquisition signals continued consolidation in the outdoor and winter sports retail sector.
