<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

At AI Engineer New York, frontier AI labs including OpenAI and Anthropic joined financial firms to explore the balance between agent autonomy and safety in high-stakes environments. Governance and control mechanisms emerged as critical considerations as AI agents move into real-world financial applications.
