<div class="lang-ko">

AI 성능을 측정하는 리테일 업계의 기존 지표인 '참여도(engagement)'는 진짜 중요한 질문을 놓치고 있다. 핵심 지표는 AI가 소비자 질문에 답변을 생성할 때 해당 브랜드나 제품이 포함(inclusion)되는지 여부다. AI 검색 및 추천 시스템에서 노출되지 않는 브랜드는 점점 더 구매 고려 대상에서 제외될 위험에 처한다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

The retail industry is measuring AI performance with the wrong metric: engagement with AI tools is not the key indicator that matters. What truly counts is whether a brand or product is included in AI-generated answers when consumers ask questions. Brands that fail to appear in AI-driven search and recommendation outputs risk being systematically excluded from the consumer consideration set.

</div>

## Key Points

- AI engagement metrics are misleading; inclusion in AI-generated answers is the true performance measure
- Brands not appearing in AI answers risk being excluded from consumer consideration funnels
- A new category of AI visibility optimization is emerging as a critical marketing discipline
- Retailers need to rethink how they structure product data and content for AI discoverability
