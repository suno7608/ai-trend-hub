<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Stripe is reportedly set to acquire AI gateway startup OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, marking a major move into AI infrastructure. OpenRouter's CEO had previously described the company as 'Stripe for AI,' highlighting its role as a unified gateway for accessing multiple AI models. The acquisition signals that AI routing and orchestration infrastructure is becoming a critical layer in the next generation of commerce and payment ecosystems.
