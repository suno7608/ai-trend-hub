<div class="lang-ko">

NBCUniversal은 'Traitors', 'Love Island USA', 'Top Chef', 'Below Deck' 등 인기 프로그램 약 12개를 통해 시청자가 TV를 보면서 바로 제품을 구매할 수 있는 쇼퍼블 TV 비즈니스를 확장하고 있다. NBCU는 자사 플랫폼인 Peacock뿐만 아니라 LG TV 등 서드파티 플랫폼에서도 쇼퍼블 TV 기능을 개발·적용하고 있다. 이는 콘텐츠 시청과 커머스를 통합하는 커넥티드 TV 광고 생태계의 성장을 보여주는 사례다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

NBCUniversal is expanding its shoppable TV business, enabling viewers to purchase products directly while watching approximately 12 popular shows including 'Top Chef,' 'Below Deck,' 'Traitors,' and 'Love Island USA.' NBCU is deploying shoppable TV capabilities across its own Peacock platform as well as third-party platforms, including LG TVs. This development highlights the accelerating integration of content viewing and commerce within the connected TV ecosystem.

</div>

- NBCU's shoppable TV spans ~12 shows across Peacock and third-party platforms including LG TVs — direct relevance to LG's platform strategy
- Hit reality and lifestyle shows are proving to be strong drivers of shoppable TV engagement
- Connected TV is emerging as a viable commerce channel, blurring lines between entertainment and retail
