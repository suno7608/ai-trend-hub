Francesca's, the women's apparel retailer, has had its Chapter 11 liquidation plan confirmed, marking the formal end of its operations. The company resolved several disputes with creditors and received no other qualified bids for its intellectual property aside from Stand Out For Good. This outcome highlights the ongoing structural challenges facing mid-market brick-and-mortar apparel retailers.
