<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

OpenAI has introduced product carousel ads to ChatGPT, adopting a Google Shopping-style visual format to enhance the shopping experience within the chatbot. This builds upon existing product feed campaign capabilities and is timed strategically ahead of the Q4 holiday shopping period. The move marks a significant step in ChatGPT's evolution from an information tool to a full-fledged commerce platform.
