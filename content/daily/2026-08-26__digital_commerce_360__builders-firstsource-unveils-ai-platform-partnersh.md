건축 자재 유통 대기업 Builders FirstSource가 AI 기반 주택 건설 플랫폼 Digs의 2,530만 달러 규모 시리즈 A 투자 라운드를 주도했다. 양사는 5년간 제품 개발 및 플랫폼 통합을 추진하는 상업적 협약을 체결했으며, Digs의 AI 기능을 Builders FirstSource 고객에게 제공할 계획이다. 이번 파트너십은 주택 건설 산업에서 AI 플랫폼 생태계 구축이 가속화되고 있음을 보여준다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Builders FirstSource has led a $25.3 million Series A financing round for Digs, an AI-powered platform serving homebuilders and homeowners, forming a strategic commercial partnership as part of the deal. The five-year agreement will focus on product development and platform integration, bringing Digs' AI capabilities to Builders FirstSource's customer base. The partnership signals growing momentum for AI-driven platform ecosystems within the home construction industry.

</div>

## Key Points

- Builders FirstSource leads a $25.3M Series A round in AI platform Digs, forming a 5-year commercial partnership
- The deal will integrate Digs' AI features into Builders FirstSource's homebuilder and homeowner ecosystem
- This represents a broader trend of industrial distributors investing in AI platform capabilities to enhance customer value
