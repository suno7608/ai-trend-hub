엔비디아 대표들이 일리노이 하원의원 빌 포스터에게 칩 보안법에서 요구하는 반밀수 추적 조치가 기술적으로 가능하다고 비공개로 확인했다. 반면 업계 단체는 공개적으로 해당 조치가 '잠재적으로 실현 불가능하다'고 주장해 온 것으로 알려졌다. 이 같은 엔비디아의 비공개 입장과 업계의 공개 입장 간 괴리가 MIT 테크놀로지 리뷰와의 인터뷰를 통해 처음 드러났다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Nvidia representatives privately told Illinois Representative Bill Foster that implementing chip-tracking anti-smuggling measures required by the Chip Security Act would be technically feasible. This contradicts the public stance of an industry group, which claimed such measures were 'potentially infeasible.' The discrepancy was revealed by Foster in an interview with MIT Technology Review.

</div>

## Key Points

- Nvidia privately confirmed chip tracking for anti-smuggling is technically feasible
- Industry group publicly claimed such measures are 'potentially infeasible', contradicting Nvidia's private stance
- The Chip Security Act is at the center of this debate over AI chip export controls
- The discrepancy was disclosed by US Representative Bill Foster to MIT Technology Review
