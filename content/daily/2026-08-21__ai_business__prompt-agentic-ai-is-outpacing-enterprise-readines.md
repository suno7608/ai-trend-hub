<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Agentic AI deployments are accelerating rapidly, but many enterprises lack the foundational processes, data infrastructure, cost controls, and governance mechanisms needed to support them at scale. The growing gap between agent deployment pace and enterprise readiness poses significant operational risks. Building internal capabilities to manage and control AI agents is emerging as a critical priority for organizations.

## Key Points

- Enterprise AI agent deployments are outpacing internal readiness across processes, data, and controls
- Key gaps include cost management, governance frameworks, and scalable operational processes
- The readiness gap increases risk of failed or uncontrolled agent deployments
- Organizations must invest in foundational infrastructure alongside agent technology adoption
