</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Francisco Vigo, co-founder of GeoSurge, warns that most of what marketers are expected to spend on AI search visibility in 2027 is misdirected. While interest in AI search optimization is growing rapidly, distinguishing between strategies that deliver genuine results and those that do not is becoming critically important. Marketers need more effective and nuanced approaches to optimizing for AI search visibility.
