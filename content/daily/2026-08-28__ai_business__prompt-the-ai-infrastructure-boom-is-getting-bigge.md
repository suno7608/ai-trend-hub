</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Nvidia's record quarter underscores that AI infrastructure demand continues to surge, with the boom expanding well beyond GPUs into CPUs, networking, robotics, and edge computing. The AI infrastructure race is shifting from a single-chip competition to a comprehensive full-stack technology contest. This signals a fundamental paradigm shift in how enterprises should think about and invest in AI technology infrastructure.
