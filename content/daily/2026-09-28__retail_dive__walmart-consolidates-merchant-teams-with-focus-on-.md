월마트가 패션, 홈, 뷰티 머천트 팀을 통합하는 조직 개편을 단행했다. 이는 약 20년 전 실패로 끝난 패션 중심의 '프로젝트 임팩트' 이후 새롭게 스타일 부문 강화를 꾀하는 전략적 움직임이다. 이번 통합은 월마트가 스타일 관련 카테고리에서 경쟁력을 높이려는 의도로 해석된다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Walmart is merging its fashion, home, and beauty merchant teams into a unified style organization. This move comes nearly 20 years after the retailer's failed fashion-focused 'Project Impact' initiative. The consolidation signals Walmart's renewed strategic push to strengthen its position in style-related product categories.

</div>

## Key Points

- Walmart is merging fashion, home, and beauty merchant teams into a single style-focused organization
- The move echoes and contrasts with the failed 'Project Impact' fashion initiative launched nearly 20 years ago
- The reorganization reflects a strategic intent to build credibility and competitiveness in style categories
