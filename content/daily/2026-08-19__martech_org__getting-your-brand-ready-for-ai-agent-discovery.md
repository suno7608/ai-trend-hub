<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

As AI agents increasingly mediate product discovery, merely appearing in an AI-generated answer is no longer sufficient—brands must be actively chosen. The article outlines how AI agents evaluate and select brands differently from traditional search engines, requiring new optimization strategies. Structured data, credibility signals, and clear brand positioning are identified as critical factors for being selected by AI agents.

## Key Points

- Being included in AI-generated answers is not enough—brands must be selected to win in agentic commerce
- AI agents use different discovery and evaluation criteria compared to traditional search algorithms
- Structured data, brand credibility signals, and clear positioning are key to AI agent selection
- Brands need a dedicated 'AI agent discoverability' strategy as a new marketing priority
