<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

GameStop forged a private deal with lenders to swap $1.4 billion in loans due in four to six years for shares of its Class A common stock. The debt-for-equity transaction is designed to preserve the retailer's cash reserves amid ongoing operational pressures. The move comes alongside reports of an eBay bid, raising questions about GameStop's broader strategic direction.
