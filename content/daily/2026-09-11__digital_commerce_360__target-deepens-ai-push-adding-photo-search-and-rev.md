<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Target has highlighted several AI-powered shopping features rolled out since 2025, signaling a deeper commitment to weaving artificial intelligence into everyday retail experiences. Key tools include Photo Search, which allows shoppers to upload images to find matching products, and Review Insights, which surfaces key takeaways from customer reviews. The retailer is also enabling session continuity so shoppers can pick up where they left off online.
