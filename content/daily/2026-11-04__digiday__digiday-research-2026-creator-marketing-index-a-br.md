According to Digiday's 2026 Creator Marketing Index, creator marketing has become a fixture in many brands' marketing strategies, with creators' roles shifting and expanding significantly. Creators are evolving from simple content producers to strategic brand partners. The report serves as a brand guide for effectively leveraging creators in social media campaigns.
