<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Fast-fashion e-commerce giant Shein has agreed to a $100 million settlement with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission over allegations of deceptive practices. The FTC accused Shein of making false fast-shipping promises and improperly posting product reviews online. This settlement highlights growing regulatory scrutiny of e-commerce platforms over consumer trust and transparency issues.
