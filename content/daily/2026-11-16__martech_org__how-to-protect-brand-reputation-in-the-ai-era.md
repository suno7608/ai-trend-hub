<div class="lang-ko">

AI 시대에 브랜드의 이야기는 검색, AI 플랫폼, 서드파티 소스 등 외부 채널을 통해 전달되는 경우가 늘고 있다. 브랜드가 직접 통제하지 못하는 환경에서도 일관된 평판을 유지하려면 선제적인 평판 관리 전략이 필요하다. 검색 엔진, AI 모델, 외부 콘텐츠 소스 전반에 걸쳐 강력한 브랜드 존재감을 구축하는 것이 핵심 과제다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

In the AI era, a brand's narrative is increasingly shaped by external forces including AI platforms, search engines, and third-party sources beyond the brand's direct control. Maintaining a consistent and strong reputation requires proactive strategies that extend well beyond owned media. Building robust brand presence across search, AI-generated responses, and third-party sources has become a critical marketing priority.

</div>

## Key Points

- Brand narratives are increasingly shaped by AI platforms and third-party sources outside brand control
- Proactive reputation management must now span search, AI, and external content ecosystems
- Consistency across AI-generated and third-party brand representations is a growing strategic imperative
