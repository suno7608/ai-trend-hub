<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Salesforce's Agentforce Blog introduced the concept of an 'AI harness' as a practical framework for small and medium-sized businesses to manage AI agents and connect them to business growth outcomes. Described as an orchestration layer for multiple AI agents, the AI harness reduces technical complexity while enabling agent-based automation at scale. Salesforce positions this framework as a way for SMBs to access enterprise-grade AI capabilities without deep technical expertise.
