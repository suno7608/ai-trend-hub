<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Target has unveiled a high-concept holiday advertising campaign centered on a 'gift-giving academy' theme, featuring actor D'Arcy Carden as the Chief Giftologist. The campaign is backed by a significantly expanded creator strategy focused on YouTube and TikTok to amplify social media reach. The approach combines narrative-driven brand advertising with the scale of the creator economy for the 2026 holiday season.
