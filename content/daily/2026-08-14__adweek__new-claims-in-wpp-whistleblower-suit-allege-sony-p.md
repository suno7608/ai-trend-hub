<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

A former WPP executive has added new claims to his whistleblower lawsuit, alleging that a Sony investigation uncovered evidence of illegal media rebate fraud at the agency. The plaintiff contends he was fired in retaliation for attempting to expose the alleged rebate scheme, and is suing WPP for wrongful termination. The high-profile case raises serious questions about media buying transparency and ethical practices within major advertising holding companies.
