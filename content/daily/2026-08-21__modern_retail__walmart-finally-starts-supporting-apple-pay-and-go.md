<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Walmart has announced it will begin supporting Apple Pay and Google Pay in its physical stores by the end of 2026, ending years of consumer frustration over the retailer's absence of near-universal mobile payment options. The move aligns Walmart with standard industry payment practices and is expected to reduce checkout friction for millions of shoppers. This decision underscores how frictionless payment experiences are increasingly critical to retail competitiveness and customer retention.

</div>

## Key Points

- Walmart will support Apple Pay and Google Pay in stores by end of 2026
- Move ends years of consumer frustration with Walmart's non-support of ubiquitous mobile payments
- Frictionless checkout is now a baseline expectation, not a differentiator, in retail
- Signals that even the largest holdout retailers can no longer afford to ignore dominant payment standards
