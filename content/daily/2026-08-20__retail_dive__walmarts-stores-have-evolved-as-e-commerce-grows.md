월마트 CFO 존 데이비드 레이니는 전자상거래 성장에 따라 오프라인 매장의 역할이 진화하고 있으며, 옴니채널 전략에서 매장이 여전히 핵심 역할을 한다고 밝혔다. 월마트의 2분기 미국 동일매장 매출 증가율은 의약품 가격 규제의 영향을 받아 다소 둔화되었다. 이는 유통 대기업도 정책 변화와 규제 환경에 민감하게 영향을 받음을 보여준다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Walmart CFO John David Rainey stated that physical stores remain vital to the retailer's strategy as the business becomes increasingly omnichannel, emphasizing that brick-and-mortar has evolved rather than declined. Walmart's Q2 U.S. comparable store sales growth was moderated by the impact of drug pricing regulation. The commentary highlights how even retail giants must navigate regulatory headwinds alongside their digital transformation efforts.

</div>

## Key Points

- Walmart CFO affirms brick-and-mortar stores have evolved and remain central to omnichannel strategy
- Q2 U.S. comp sales growth was softened by drug pricing regulation impact
- Omnichannel integration—not pure e-commerce—is Walmart's stated retail model
- Regulatory factors can materially impact even the largest retailers' financial performance
