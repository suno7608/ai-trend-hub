Cadbury launched its largest-ever US campaign titled 'There's Magic in More,' pairing a crystal clutch product with a branded comedy series. The campaign includes a multichannel media buy alongside a New York Fashion Week activation. This integrated approach signals a significant investment in brand awareness and cultural relevance in the US market.
