<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Amazon's Prime Day is catalyzing increased advertiser investment in Amazon Video and DSP (Demand-Side Platform) channels, with brands finding it easy to justify the spend to finance leadership. Advertisers remain cautious about deep discounting that erodes profit margins, yet continue to grow their Amazon ad budgets due to its strong performance correlation. This signals Amazon's retail media network becoming a default, CFO-approved component of brand marketing strategies.

- Amazon's retail media ecosystem is consolidating its role as a primary brand marketing channel
