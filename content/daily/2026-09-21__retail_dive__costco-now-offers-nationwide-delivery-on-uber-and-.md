<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Costco has expanded its nationwide partnerships with Uber Eats and DoorDash to offer same-day delivery as consumer demand for rapid fulfillment continues to grow. The move integrates the warehouse retailer's physical scale with on-demand delivery infrastructure. This partnership reflects a broader industry trend of brick-and-mortar retailers leveraging third-party delivery platforms to compete in e-commerce.
