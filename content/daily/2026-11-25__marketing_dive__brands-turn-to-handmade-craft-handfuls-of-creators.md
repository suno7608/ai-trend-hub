<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Major brands including Coca-Cola, Walmart, and Levi's are shifting their holiday marketing strategies away from AI-generated content toward handmade aesthetics and authentic human creators. Consumer fatigue with AI-generated ads is driving this pivot as brands seek to restore emotional connection. This trend signals a potential backlash against over-reliance on AI in marketing creative.
