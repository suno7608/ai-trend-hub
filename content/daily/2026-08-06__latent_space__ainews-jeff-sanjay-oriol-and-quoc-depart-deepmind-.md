Google DeepMind is experiencing a significant leadership upheaval with the departure of several founding-era researchers including Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le. Demis Hassabis is transitioning to a Chair role while Koray Kavukcuoglu is being elevated to SVP, signaling a structural reorganization. This marks a potential end-of-era shift for one of the most influential AI research organizations in the world.
