<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Jell-O, the iconic Kraft Heinz brand, has launched its largest marketing campaign in more than a decade, developed by agency Johannes Leonardo as its first work for the brand. The campaign centers on a new creative platform designed to fundamentally shift consumer perceptions of the brand. This marks a significant strategic pivot for Jell-O, which has seen reduced marketing investment over many years.
