Ross Stores posted a remarkable 10% same-store sales increase in Q2 2026, significantly outperforming major rival TJX Companies. The growth was driven by both new and returning customers flocking to the off-price retailer. This performance highlights the growing consumer preference for value-oriented retail amid economic uncertainty.
