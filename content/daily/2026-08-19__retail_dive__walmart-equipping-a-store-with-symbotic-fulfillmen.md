<div class="lang-ko">

월마트가 Symbotic의 SymMicro 자동화 풀필먼트 시스템을 매장 내에 도입한다고 발표했다. 이 시스템은 약 6개월 내 가동될 예정이며, 매장 내 픽업 및 배송 운영 가속화를 목표로 한다. 이는 월마트가 공급망 자동화와 라스트마일 물류 경쟁력 강화에 적극 투자하고 있음을 보여준다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Walmart is equipping a store with Symbotic's SymMicro automated fulfillment system, expected to be operational within approximately six months. The deployment aims to accelerate in-store pickup and delivery operations, reinforcing Walmart's commitment to supply chain automation. This move reflects the growing trend of retailers embedding robotics and AI-driven logistics directly within physical store locations.

</div>

## Key Points

- Walmart is installing Symbotic's SymMicro system inside a store to automate fulfillment
- System is expected to go live in approximately six months
- Goal is to speed up in-store pickup and delivery operations
- Reflects broader retail trend of embedding AI-driven robotics within physical store infrastructure
