<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Revlon has named musician and actress Teyana Taylor and country music star Megan Moroney as the faces of its 'Revlon Be Unforgettable' campaign as part of a brand image refresh. Rather than distancing itself from its heritage, Revlon is leaning into its legacy while modernizing its visual and cultural identity. The diverse ambassador selection signals an effort to broaden its consumer appeal across different demographics.
