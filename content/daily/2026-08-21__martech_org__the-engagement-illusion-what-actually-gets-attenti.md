While AI has dramatically accelerated content production, earning genuine consumer attention has become harder than ever, creating what the article calls an 'engagement illusion.' Vanity metrics like clicks and impressions increasingly mask a failure to generate meaningful audience connection. MarTech is hosting a discussion on September 2 to address how marketers can cut through the noise and create content that truly resonates.

## Key Points

- AI content abundance is creating an 'engagement illusion'—high output but declining true attention
- Standard engagement metrics (clicks, impressions) are increasingly unreliable indicators of actual attention
- Brands must differentiate through content quality and relevance, not just production speed
- MarTech September 2 event to explore frameworks for meaningful engagement in an AI-saturated landscape
