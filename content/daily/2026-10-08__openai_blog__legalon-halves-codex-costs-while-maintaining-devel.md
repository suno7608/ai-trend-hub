Legal AI startup LegalOn reduced estimated daily Codex costs by 65% while maintaining development speed through strategic model allocation. The company matched different AI models—Astra, Sol, and Luna—to specific task types and managed budgets systematically. This case highlights cost optimization as a critical factor in scaling enterprise AI adoption sustainably.
