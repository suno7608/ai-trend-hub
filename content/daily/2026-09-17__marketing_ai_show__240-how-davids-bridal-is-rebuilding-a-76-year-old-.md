데이비드 브라이달은 75년간 드레스 중심 비즈니스에서 벗어나 AI 웨딩 플래너 'Pearl Planner'를 중심으로 전체 결혼 여정을 아우르는 '에이슬 투 알고리즘(aisle to algorithm)' 전략으로 전환하고 있다. Pearl Planner는 별도의 마케팅 비용 없이 수십만 커플에게 채택된 무료 AI 웨딩 플래닝 컴패니언으로, Google Cloud 기반의 벤더 플랫폼 Pearl Connect와 연동된다. 비전 퀴즈와 동적 예산 책정 기능을 포함한 이 AI 생태계는 내부 팀의 업무 방식도 변화시키고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

David's Bridal is transforming from a 75-year-old dress-centric retailer into a full wedding journey platform powered by AI, adopting an 'aisle to algorithm' strategy. Its AI wedding-planning companion, Pearl Planner, has been adopted by hundreds of thousands of couples with zero marketing spend, supported by a vendor-side platform called Pearl Connect built on Google Cloud. Features including a vision quiz and dynamic budgeting, along with internal AI adoption, are driving a comprehensive business reinvention.

</div>

## Key Points

- Pearl Planner acquired hundreds of thousands of users with no marketing spend, proving AI companions as organic growth tools
- 'Aisle to algorithm' strategy pivots the brand from product-centric to full customer journey coverage
- Pearl Connect vendor platform built on Google Cloud extends the ecosystem to the supply side
- Internal AI adoption is accelerating alongside customer-facing transformation
