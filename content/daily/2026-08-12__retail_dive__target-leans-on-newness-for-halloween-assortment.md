Target is leaning heavily on newness for its 2026 Halloween assortment, with a majority of the festive collection featuring new items. The retailer is positioning fresh merchandise as a central pillar of its broader business turnaround strategy. This approach signals Target's intent to differentiate through exclusive and novel product offerings to drive customer engagement.
