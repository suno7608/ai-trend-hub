<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Lettuce growers such as Little Leaf Farms and salad chains like Sweetgreen are using social media to reassure consumers that their products are unaffected by the ongoing cyclospora outbreak. The brands are proactively communicating food safety information directly to consumers as a crisis management strategy. This represents a D2C-style approach to crisis communications, bypassing traditional media to restore consumer confidence quickly.

## Key Points

- Little Leaf Farms and Sweetgreen are using social media to distance themselves from the cyclospora outbreak
- Direct-to-consumer crisis communication via social channels is emerging as a key brand protection tactic
- Proactive awareness campaigns help brands avoid reputational spillover from broader industry crises
