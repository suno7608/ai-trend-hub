<div class="lang-ko">

가트너에 따르면 많은 마케터들이 2030년까지 하위 직급 역할을 AI로 대체할 수 있게 될 전망이다. 그러나 가트너는 역할을 없애는 대신 신입 직원에게 더 큰 책임을 부여하는 방식을 권장한다. AI가 마케팅 업무 구조를 근본적으로 바꾸면서 CMO들은 인재 채용 전략을 전면 재검토해야 하는 시점이다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Gartner research indicates that many marketers will be able to eliminate bottom-rung roles by 2030 as AI takes over routine tasks. However, Gartner recommends that instead of cutting entry-level positions, organizations should assign new hires greater responsibility and strategic work. CMOs are urged to fundamentally rethink their talent acquisition and development strategies in light of AI's growing capabilities.

</div>

## Key Points

- Gartner predicts AI could eliminate many entry-level marketing roles by 2030
- Recommended approach: elevate new hires to higher-responsibility roles rather than eliminating headcount
- CMOs must redesign talent strategy to align with AI-augmented marketing workflows
