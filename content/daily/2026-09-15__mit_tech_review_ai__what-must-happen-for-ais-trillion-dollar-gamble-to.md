<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

MIT Technology Review examines the conditions necessary for the massive trillion-dollar wave of AI infrastructure investment to generate genuine economic returns. Wharton finance professor Jessica Wachter highlights the complexity of business and technical uncertainties surrounding AI's economic impact, noting the concentration of AI investment among a handful of companies as a key starting fact. The article raises substantive concerns about the gap between AI investment scale and the actual delivery of measurable economic value.

</div>

## Key Points

- Trillions of dollars are being invested in AI infrastructure with uncertain economic return timelines
- AI investment is heavily concentrated among a small number of dominant companies, creating systemic risk
- Business and technical uncertainties make it difficult to forecast AI's near-term economic impact
- The gap between AI capital expenditure and demonstrated economic value is a growing concern for investors and enterprises
