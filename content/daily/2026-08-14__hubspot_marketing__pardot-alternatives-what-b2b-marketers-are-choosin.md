With 70% of marketers believing the industry has changed more in the past three years than the past 50, demand for advanced marketing automation has surged. HubSpot highlights the growing market shift away from Pardot and outlines competing platforms now favored by B2B marketers.

- Pardot은 개발 정체로 인해 B2B 마케팅 자동화 시장에서 점유율을 잃고 있음
