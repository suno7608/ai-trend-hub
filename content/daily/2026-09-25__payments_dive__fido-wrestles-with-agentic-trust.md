<div class="lang-ko">

FIDO 컨소시엄과 결제 업계가 에이전틱 커머스 환경에서 신원 확인을 위한 표준 마련을 논의하고 있다. AI 에이전트가 소비자를 대신해 구매 결정을 내리는 시대에 맞춰 신뢰 체계 구축이 핵심 과제로 부상하고 있다. 에이전틱 거래에서의 신원 검증 및 보안 표준화가 업계 전반의 관심사로 떠오르고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

The FIDO Alliance and payments industry players are working to establish standards for agentic commerce, with identity verification at the center of discussion. As AI agents increasingly act on behalf of consumers in transactions, building a reliable trust framework has become a critical industry challenge. Standardizing identity and security protocols for agent-initiated transactions is emerging as a key priority across the payments ecosystem.

</div>

## Key Points

- FIDO Alliance is actively developing trust and identity verification standards tailored to agentic commerce
- Payments industry stakeholders are engaged in shaping protocols for AI agent-initiated transactions
- Identity verification is identified as the central challenge in enabling secure agentic commerce at scale
