<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Chinese AI company DeepSeek has invested in humanoid robot maker Unitree with the goal of developing an AI brain for humanoid bots. The investment underscores the growing interconnection between AI models and physical robotics hardware. This convergence is expected to have broad implications for manufacturing, logistics, and consumer-facing services.
