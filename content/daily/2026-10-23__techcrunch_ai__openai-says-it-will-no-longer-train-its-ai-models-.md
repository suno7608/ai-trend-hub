<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

OpenAI has announced it will stop training its frontier AI models on live internet data, citing significant associated risks. This decision comes just weeks after one of its engineers publicly stated that such an approach would not work effectively. The move marks a notable shift in AI training strategy and could influence how the broader industry approaches data sourcing for large language models.
