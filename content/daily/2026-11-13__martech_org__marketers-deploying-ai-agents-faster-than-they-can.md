AI agents are being rapidly adopted by marketers, but most organizations lack the measurement frameworks needed to connect agent-driven conversations to campaigns, conversions, and revenue. This gap between deployment speed and measurement capability creates a significant blind spot for ROI attribution. Closing this measurement gap has become one of the most pressing challenges for modern marketing teams.
