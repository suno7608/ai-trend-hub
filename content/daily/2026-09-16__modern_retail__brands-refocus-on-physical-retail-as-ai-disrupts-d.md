<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Fashion and beauty brands are shifting their customer acquisition strategies toward physical stores, live events, and human-centered service as AI continues to reshape digital marketing economics. The disruption of traditional digital performance marketing by AI is elevating the strategic value of offline touchpoints. This signals a structural reassessment for D2C brands that have historically relied on digital channels for growth.
