<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

At MAICON 2026, content marketing pioneer Joe Pulizzi highlighted that AI can now generate more content in a single afternoon than most marketing teams used to produce in a month. He argued that in an era of AI-driven content abundance, trust must evolve from a value to a core strategic pillar for marketers. Authenticity and credibility, rather than volume, are emerging as the decisive differentiators for brands navigating the AI content era.
