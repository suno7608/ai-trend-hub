OpenAI가 AI 기반 광고 경험을 재정의하는 새로운 솔루션을 발표했으며, 여기에는 스폰서드 에이전트(Sponsored Agents), 마케터 도구, HubSpot 및 Shopify와의 통합이 포함된다. 이는 AI 에이전트가 광고 노출뿐 아니라 실제 구매 여정에 직접 개입하는 에이전틱 커머스 시대의 개막을 알린다. 광고주는 AI 에이전트를 통해 소비자와의 접점을 새로운 방식으로 확보할 수 있게 된다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

OpenAI has announced new AI-powered advertising experiences, including Sponsored Agents, dedicated tools for marketers, and integrations with HubSpot and Shopify. This marks a significant shift toward agentic commerce, where AI agents not only surface ads but actively participate in the consumer purchase journey. The initiative positions OpenAI as a major player in the next generation of AI-native advertising infrastructure.

## Key Points

- OpenAI launched Sponsored Agents, enabling brands to appear within AI agent interactions
- Integrations with HubSpot and Shopify connect AI advertising directly to CRM and e-commerce workflows
- This represents a fundamental shift from passive ad delivery to active AI-mediated purchase journeys
- OpenAI is positioning itself as core infrastructure for the next generation of AI-native advertising
