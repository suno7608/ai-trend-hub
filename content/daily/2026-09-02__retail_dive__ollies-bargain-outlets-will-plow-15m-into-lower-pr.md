<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Ollie's Bargain Outlet announced a $15 million investment directed toward lowering prices as it faces falling Q2 comparable store sales in a highly competitive and challenging consumer environment. The move is aimed at defending its low-price positioning against intensifying competition in the discount retail segment. The decision reflects a broader pattern among value retailers prioritizing price leadership during periods of consumer spending pressure.
