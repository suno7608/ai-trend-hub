NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang announced his resignation on November 12, 2026, after 33 years leading the company, with CFO Colette Kress stepping in as interim CEO. The leadership change raises significant questions about the future direction of NVIDIA and potential implications for open-source AI development and American AI leadership globally. As the dominant force in AI chip infrastructure, NVIDIA's leadership transition is expected to have far-reaching effects across the AI industry.
