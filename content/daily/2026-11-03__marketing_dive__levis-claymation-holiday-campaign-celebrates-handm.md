리바이스는 AI 생성 콘텐츠의 범람에 대응하여 클레이메이션(점토 애니메이션) 기법을 활용한 핸드메이드 감성의 홀리데이 캠페인을 선보였다. 이 캠페인은 CEO 미셸 가스가 기대에 미치지 못했다고 인정한 백투스쿨 캠페인 이후 브랜드 모멘텀 회복을 위해 기획됐다. AI 콘텐츠 과잉 시대에 인간적 장인정신을 강조하는 차별화 전략을 취했다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Levi's launched a nostalgic claymation-style holiday campaign emphasizing handmade craftsmanship as a deliberate counterpoint to the growing prevalence of AI-generated content. The campaign follows a back-to-school effort that CEO Michelle Gass acknowledged failed to generate expected demand and traffic. The brand is using authentic, artisanal aesthetics to reconnect with consumers and restore brand momentum.

</div>

## Key Points

- Levi's chose claymation to deliberately contrast with AI-generated content trends
- The campaign follows a disappointing back-to-school campaign that underperformed on demand and traffic
- CEO Michelle Gass acknowledged the prior campaign's failure, signaling a strategic creative shift
- Handmade aesthetics are positioned as a brand authenticity signal in an AI-saturated media landscape
