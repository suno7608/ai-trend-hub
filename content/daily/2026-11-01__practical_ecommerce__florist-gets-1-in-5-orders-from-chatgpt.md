스페인 화훼 쇼핑몰 Persa Flores는 전체 주문의 약 20%가 ChatGPT를 통해 유입되고 있다고 밝혔다. ChatGPT를 통해 방문한 쇼핑객 대부분은 도시별 배송 페이지를 통해 유입된 것으로 나타났다. 이는 AI 챗봇이 소비자의 실제 구매 경로에서 유의미한 트래픽 소스로 부상하고 있음을 보여주는 실증 사례다.

<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Spain-based florist Persa Flores reports that approximately 1 in 5 of its orders now originate from ChatGPT-driven traffic. Most ChatGPT-referred shoppers landed on the retailer's city-specific delivery pages. This real-world case demonstrates that AI chatbots are emerging as a meaningful and measurable traffic and conversion source for e-commerce businesses.

</div>

## Key Points

- Persa Flores attributes ~20% of orders to ChatGPT referrals — a concrete proof point for AI-driven commerce
- City-specific delivery landing pages were the primary entry point for ChatGPT-referred shoppers
- Signals a shift in consumer discovery behavior from traditional search to conversational AI
- Practical case study for small-to-mid-size e-commerce businesses evaluating AI traffic channels
