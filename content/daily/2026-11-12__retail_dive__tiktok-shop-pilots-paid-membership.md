TikTok Shop has launched a pilot paid membership tier for 1 million shoppers, offering free shipping and exclusive discounts. The move mirrors loyalty program strategies seen at platforms like Amazon Prime, aiming to build a committed customer base within TikTok's social commerce ecosystem. This signals TikTok Shop's ambition to deepen its role as a full-stack commerce platform beyond entertainment-driven impulse purchases.
