<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Nvidia has acquired Hugging Face, the widely-used open-source AI platform, marking a significant strategic win in the AI race. While attention has largely focused on model makers like Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google, Nvidia is emerging as the dominant force in the AI ecosystem. The acquisition positions Nvidia to control both the hardware infrastructure and the model development community simultaneously.
