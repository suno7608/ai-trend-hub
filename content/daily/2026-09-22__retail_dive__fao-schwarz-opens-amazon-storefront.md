FAO Schwarz has opened an official storefront on Amazon to expand its online retail presence. In addition, the toy retailer will feature an Amazon pop-up shop at its flagship store in Rockefeller Center, New York. This partnership represents an omnichannel strategy combining offline brand heritage with online distribution expansion.
