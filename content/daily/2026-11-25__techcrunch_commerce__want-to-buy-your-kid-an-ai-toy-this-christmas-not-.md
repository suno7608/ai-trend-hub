New York State has enacted a five-year ban on AI chatbot toys for children following alarming findings from Common Sense Media research. The research revealed serious safety failures including children's exposure to inappropriate content and risks to emotional development. This regulatory action sets a significant precedent for the broader AI consumer products market, particularly those targeting children.
