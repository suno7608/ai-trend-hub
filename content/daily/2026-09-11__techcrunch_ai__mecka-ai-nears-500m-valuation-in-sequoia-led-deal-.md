<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Robot training data startup Mecka AI is approaching a $500 million valuation in a Sequoia-led funding round, reflecting surging investor demand for physical AI and robotics infrastructure. The two-year-old company is closing this round just months after announcing its Series A, highlighting rapid growth in the robot training data market. This investment signals that high-quality training data for physical AI systems is emerging as a critical competitive resource.
