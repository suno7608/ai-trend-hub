<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Home Depot has rolled out nationwide express delivery in 3 hours or less across the United States, leveraging its network of more than 2,000 stores as distributed fulfillment hubs. The service covers thousands of SKUs, transforming existing store infrastructure into a last-mile delivery asset. This move signals that rapid delivery is becoming a baseline expectation, raising the bar for all retail and D2C operators.

## Key Points

- Home Depot launched 3-hour express delivery nationwide across the US
- Over 2,000 stores serve as distributed fulfillment hubs for the service
- Thousands of SKUs are covered, making this a broad rather than niche offering
- The move raises consumer expectations for delivery speed across all retail categories
