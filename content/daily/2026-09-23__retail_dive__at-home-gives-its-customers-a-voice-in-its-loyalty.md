At Home, a home décor retailer, revamped its loyalty program by leveraging customer research, focus groups, and behavioral testing. The initiative was designed to give customers a direct voice in shaping the program's evolution. The approach reflects a data-driven, customer-centric strategy for improving loyalty and retention.
