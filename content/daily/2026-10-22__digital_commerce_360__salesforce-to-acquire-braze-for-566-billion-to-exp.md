<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Salesforce announced a definitive agreement to acquire Braze for $5.66 billion, offering shareholders $47.50 per share in cash, representing approximately a 58% premium over Braze's October 19 closing price. The acquisition is aimed at expanding Salesforce's AI-powered marketing technology stack. The deal focuses on enabling brands to respond to shopper behavior in real time using artificial intelligence.
