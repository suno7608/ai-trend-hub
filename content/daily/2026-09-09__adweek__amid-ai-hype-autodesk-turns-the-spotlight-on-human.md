<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Autodesk has launched a new marketing campaign that places real customers — positioned as human makers — at the center of an emotional pitch, deliberately countering the prevailing AI hype. Rather than leading with AI capabilities, the campaign emphasizes human creativity and craftsmanship as the core brand message. The approach represents a contrarian positioning strategy in a market saturated with AI-first messaging.

- Autodesk's campaign leads with human creativity rather than AI capabilities, a deliberate counter to industry trends
- Real customers ('makers') are the emotional core of the campaign narrative
- The strategy demonstrates that brands can build differentiation by humanizing in an AI-saturated market
- Emotional storytelling is positioned as a competitive tool against technology-led messaging
