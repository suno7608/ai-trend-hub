</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Effective DOOH (Digital Out-of-Home) advertising campaigns depend more on precise audience data than simply securing high-traffic screen placements. Understanding which screens actually reach the intended target audience is the key to maximizing DOOH investment efficiency. The article argues that an audience-first approach should be the foundation of any smart DOOH campaign strategy.
