<div class="lang-ko">

리바이스는 2026 회계연도 3분기(8월 30일 마감)에 이커머스 매출이 전년 대비 10% 증가했다고 발표했다. 자사 매장과 온라인 채널을 포함한 DTC 전체 매출은 2% 성장에 그쳤으나, 이커머스 부문이 DTC 성장을 주도했다. 미국 시장의 부진에도 불구하고 리바이스는 홀리데이 시즌 DTC 채널에서 더 강한 성장을 기대하고 있다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Levi Strauss & Co. reported a 10% increase in ecommerce revenue in its fiscal Q3 ended August 30, 2026, outpacing its broader DTC business which grew only 2% year over year. Ecommerce is emerging as the primary growth engine within Levi's DTC strategy, even as the US market showed weakness. The company is optimistic about stronger holiday season performance through its direct channels.

</div>

## Key Points

- Levi's ecommerce revenue grew 10% YoY in fiscal Q3 2026, significantly outpacing overall DTC growth of 2%
- US market weakness was noted, suggesting geographic variation in DTC performance
- Levi's is targeting stronger holiday season DTC sales, highlighting the strategic importance of the Q4 period for direct channels
