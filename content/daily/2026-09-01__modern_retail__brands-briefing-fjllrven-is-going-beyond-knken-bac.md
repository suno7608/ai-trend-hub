<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Swedish outdoor brand Fjällräven is expanding its retail presence in North America, opening new stores that go beyond its signature Kånken backpack to establish a broader outerwear identity. The brand is introducing a new retail concept that incorporates its sister brands, aiming to deepen its foothold in the competitive outdoor apparel market. This move reflects a broader D2C strategy of using physical retail to diversify brand experience and drive customer engagement.
