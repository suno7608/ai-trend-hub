<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Authentic Brands Group (ABG) has appointed Lark-Marie Antón from USA Today Co. as its newly created Chief Communications Officer. The role reflects ABG's strategic push into media, entertainment, and live events, which now account for approximately 20% of its business. The executive hire signals ABG's intent to build a stronger communications infrastructure to support its expanding media and brand portfolio.

</div>

## Key Points

- ABG created a new CCO role and appointed Lark-Marie Antón from USA Today Co.
- Media, entertainment, and live events now represent ~20% of ABG's business
- The move reflects ABG's strategic diversification beyond traditional brand licensing
