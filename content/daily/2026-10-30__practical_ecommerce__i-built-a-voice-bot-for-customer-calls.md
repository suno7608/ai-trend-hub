<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

D2C grooming brand Beardbrand developed an AI voice bot powered by synthetic voices and AI agents to address the burden of customer phone support. The voice bot automates inbound customer call handling with the goal of improving operational efficiency. This case illustrates a practical approach for D2C brands to reduce customer service costs and scale support capacity using AI agents.

## Key Points

- Beardbrand built an AI voice bot using synthetic voices to automate customer phone support
- Customer phone support was identified as a significant operational burden for the D2C brand
- AI agents enable scalable, cost-efficient customer service without expanding human headcount
- Demonstrates that AI-powered voice automation is accessible for mid-size D2C brands
