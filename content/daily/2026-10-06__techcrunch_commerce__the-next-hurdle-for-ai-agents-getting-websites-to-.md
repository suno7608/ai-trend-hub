<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Personal AI agents promise to handle tasks like shopping, booking flights, and making reservations on behalf of consumers, but deliberate website blocks and anti-bot defenses are creating significant friction. These barriers are leaving consumers caught in the middle, unable to fully benefit from AI agent capabilities. A new industry standard is emerging to address the access conflict between AI agents and websites.

- AI personal agents capable of shopping and booking are being blocked by existing anti-bot website defenses
- Consumers are negatively impacted as they cannot fully leverage AI agent capabilities due to these restrictions
- A new industry standard is being developed to establish protocols for AI agent access to websites
- The tension between website security and AI agent accessibility is a defining challenge for the next phase of e-commerce
