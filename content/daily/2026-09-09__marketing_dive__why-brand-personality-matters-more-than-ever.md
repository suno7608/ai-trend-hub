<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Gartner's Julie Reeves uses Callaway Golf's recent marketing misstep as a cautionary tale about the need for clearly defined brand personality guardrails. As AI scales content production, maintaining a consistent brand identity becomes increasingly challenging without structured guidelines. Brands that fail to establish these guardrails risk losing coherence across their marketing output.

- Callaway Golf's marketing misstep illustrates the risk of unclear brand personality guidelines
