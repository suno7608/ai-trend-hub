<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Salesforce's Agentforce blog argues that not all bot traffic is harmful, urging businesses to develop nuanced bot mitigation strategies in the age of AI crawlers. Historically, bots were primarily associated with scrapers, scalpers, and credential stuffers, but the rise of AI agents is fundamentally changing the bot traffic landscape. The article outlines how organizations can strategically differentiate between legitimate AI bots and malicious ones.

## Key Points

- AI crawlers are reshaping the bot traffic landscape, requiring more nuanced defense strategies
- Traditional bot threats (scrapers, scalpers, credential stuffers) are now joined by legitimate AI agents
- Salesforce advocates for distinguishing between beneficial AI bots and malicious traffic rather than blanket blocking
- Businesses need updated bot mitigation frameworks that account for agentic commerce use cases
