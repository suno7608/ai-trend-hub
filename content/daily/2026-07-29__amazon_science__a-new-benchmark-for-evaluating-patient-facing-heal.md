<div class="lang-ko">

아마존 사이언스가 환자 대면 의료 AI 에이전트를 평가하기 위한 새로운 벤치마크인 'PatientAgentBench'를 공개했다. 이 벤치마크는 합성 환자 건강 기록, 현실적인 임상 시나리오, 환자 에이전트를 생성하여 AI 시스템이 실제로 수행해야 할 작업을 평가한다. 의료 AI 에이전트의 신뢰성과 안전성 평가 기준 마련에 기여할 것으로 기대된다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Amazon Science has introduced PatientAgentBench, a new benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents in patient-facing healthcare settings. The benchmark generates synthetic patient health records, realistic clinical vignettes, and a patient agent that converses with the AI system being evaluated, capturing the real tasks a patient-facing agent must perform. This initiative aims to establish rigorous evaluation standards for the reliability and safety of health AI agents.

</div>

## Key Points

- Amazon Science launched PatientAgentBench to standardize evaluation of patient-facing health AI agents
- The benchmark uses synthetic health records and clinical vignettes to simulate real patient-agent interactions
- This contributes to establishing safety and reliability standards for AI agents in high-stakes domains
- Benchmarking methodology may influence how AI agent quality is assessed across other verticals
