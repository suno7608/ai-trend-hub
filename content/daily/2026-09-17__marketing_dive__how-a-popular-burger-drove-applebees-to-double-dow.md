<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Applebee's is expanding its 'O-M-Cheese' marketing platform following the success of a popular cheese burger, with CMO Michelle Chin explaining how the chain is leveraging brand partnerships to highlight what makes the platform unique. The strategy connects menu innovation directly to a cohesive marketing platform identity. This approach illustrates how food and beverage brands are building scalable marketing platforms around singular product insights.
