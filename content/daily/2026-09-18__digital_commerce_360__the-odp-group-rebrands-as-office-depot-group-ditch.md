<div class="lang-ko">

ODP Group이 Office Depot Group으로 공식 리브랜딩하며 40년 역사의 Office Depot 브랜드를 소매와 B2B 유통 사업 전반에 통합했다. 기존 B2B 유통 부문의 명칭인 'ODP Business Solutions'는 'Office Depot Business'로 변경되었다. 이번 리브랜딩은 브랜드 일관성을 강화하고 소비자 및 기업 고객 모두에게 명확한 브랜드 아이덴티티를 제공하기 위한 전략적 결정이다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

The ODP Group has officially rebranded as Office Depot Group, unifying its 40-year-old Office Depot brand across both retail stores and B2B distribution operations. The B2B distribution arm, formerly called ODP Business Solutions, has been renamed Office Depot Business to align with the parent brand. Company officials stated the move is designed to create a consistent brand identity for both consumer and business customers.

</div>

## Key Points

- ODP Group officially rebrands to Office Depot Group to unify brand identity
- ODP Business Solutions renamed to Office Depot Business for B2B operations
- Rebranding aligns 40-year-old Office Depot brand across retail and distribution
- Move intended to simplify brand messaging for both consumer and corporate audiences
