<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Social club The Den uses ChatGPT Work to free up 10–15 hours per week, enabling staff to focus on growth as the business opens a new location. Grant application preparation has been reduced from 3 days to 2 hours, and liquor-license materials from 4 days to 3 hours. The case illustrates how AI tools can dramatically reduce administrative burden for small and mid-sized businesses.
