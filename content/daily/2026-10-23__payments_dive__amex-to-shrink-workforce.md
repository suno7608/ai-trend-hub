<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

American Express plans to cut 4.5% of its workforce as the company shifts toward greater use of artificial intelligence, CEO Stephen Squeri announced. The reduction is framed as a move to make the organization more agile and efficient in an AI-driven operating environment. This signals a broader trend in financial services where AI adoption is directly reshaping workforce structures.
