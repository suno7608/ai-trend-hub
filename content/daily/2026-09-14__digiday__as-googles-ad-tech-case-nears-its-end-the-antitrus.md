<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Google has navigated its ad tech antitrust case without facing a corporate breakup, but regulatory scrutiny is now shifting toward AI advertising and connected TV (CTV). Regulators are expected to target Google's growing dominance in AI-powered ad systems and CTV ecosystems as the next frontiers of antitrust enforcement. This signals a broader regulatory realignment around AI's role in advertising market competition.
