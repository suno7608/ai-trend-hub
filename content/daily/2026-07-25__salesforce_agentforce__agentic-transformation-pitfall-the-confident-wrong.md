Salesforce's Agentforce Blog highlights a critical pitfall in agentic AI transformation: AI agents can produce confident but factually incorrect answers when lacking proper context. ## Key Points

- AI agents risk generating confident but incorrect answers without proper context — a key deployment pitfall
- Salesforce recommends certified data, AI Skills, and Tableau MCP as solutions to improve agent answer accuracy
- Data quality and context design are more critical than model capability for trustworthy agentic AI
- This is a practical warning for enterprises accelerating agentic AI rollouts in customer-facing scenarios
