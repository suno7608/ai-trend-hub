아마존은 AI 에이전트가 실제 비즈니스 표준운영절차(SOP)를 얼마나 잘 수행하는지 평가하는 새로운 벤치마크 'SOP-Bench'를 공개했다. 이 프레임워크는 단편적인 프록시 과제가 아닌, 절차 완수에 필요한 전체 역량을 테스트할 수 있도록 설계되었다. 확장 가능한 구조로 다양한 비즈니스 환경에 적용 가능하다.

</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Amazon has introduced SOP-Bench, a new benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents on real-world business standard operating procedures. Unlike traditional benchmarks that test isolated proxy tasks, SOP-Bench assesses the full range of capabilities required to complete an end-to-end procedure. The extendable framework is intended to support testing across diverse enterprise environments.

</div>

## Key Points

- SOP-Bench evaluates AI agents on complete business procedures, not isolated subtasks
- The framework is extendable, enabling testing across varied enterprise use cases
- Developed by Amazon Science, signaling major platform vendors are formalizing agent evaluation standards
- Addresses a critical gap in enterprise AI deployment: reliable procedure-level performance measurement
