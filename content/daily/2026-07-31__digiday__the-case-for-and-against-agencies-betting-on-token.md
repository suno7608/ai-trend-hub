</div>
<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Advertising agencies are exploring whether to purchase and negotiate AI compute costs (tokens) at scale, similar to how they historically managed media buying upfronts. AI compute is emerging as a new frontier for agency deal-making and cost optimization. While this model could unlock new revenue streams for agencies, it also carries significant financial risk.
