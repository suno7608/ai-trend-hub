E-commerce marketing automation platform Klaviyo has acquired the AI agency founded by serial entrepreneur Elias Torres, appointing him as Chief Product Officer to lead its AI agents initiative. The acquisition signals Klaviyo's strategic elevation of AI agent capabilities as a core product priority. Torres, a well-known figure in the marketing technology space, is expected to significantly accelerate Klaviyo's AI agent development.
