<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

According to Deloitte's annual holiday shopping survey, 22% of respondents plan to start their holiday shopping earlier than last year. An estimated 28% of shoppers' budgets are expected to be exhausted by the end of October, the highest level recorded in six years. This trend carries significant implications for supply chain planning and inventory management.

</div>

## Key Points

- 22% of consumers plan to start holiday shopping earlier than the previous year
- 28% of holiday budgets projected to be spent by end of October — a 6-year high
- Early shopping behavior creates both supply chain pressures and early revenue opportunities for D2C brands
- Brands that activate promotions and inventory readiness in Q3 stand to capture disproportionate share of early spend
