<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

M&M's has launched 'Screentime,' a campaign created by BBDO Chicago that places its iconic candy mascots into parodies of crime thrillers, telenovelas, and the reality show 'Survivor.' The campaign leverages the brand's well-established mascot equity by blending it with popular film and TV genre tropes for humorous effect. It represents a content marketing strategy aimed at deepening brand affinity through culturally resonant storytelling.
