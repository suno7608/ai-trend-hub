<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Small-town public records offices across America are being overwhelmed by a surge in AI-assisted open records requests. Town clerks like Kyle Rowe in Panton, Vermont, who already juggle multiple administrative roles, are struggling to keep up with the volume. The trend highlights unintended societal consequences of AI tooling beyond commercial applications.
