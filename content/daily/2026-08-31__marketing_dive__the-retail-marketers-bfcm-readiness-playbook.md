A new BFCM readiness playbook has been released to help retail marketers identify gaps in their strategies before the peak holiday shopping season arrives. The guide emphasizes proactively auditing brand capabilities and marketing infrastructure ahead of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. It provides practical checklists and strategic direction to ensure brands are fully prepared for high-traffic sales periods.
