<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Bed Bath & Beyond reported back-to-back quarters of revenue growth in its fiscal Q2 2026, signaling a recovery trajectory. The company announced a full rebrand to 'Neighborhood Intelligence,' relocating to Nashville and preparing to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker NXH starting August 17. The transformation reflects a broader strategic pivot beyond its retail origins.
