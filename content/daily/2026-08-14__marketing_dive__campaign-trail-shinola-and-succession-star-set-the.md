<div class="lang-en" style="display:none">

Detroit-based design brand Shinola has launched its first-ever brand campaign, created with agency GentleForces and featuring actor Nicholas Braun of 'Succession', who also directed the ad. The campaign adopts an anxiety-themed creative direction to build brand awareness and emotional resonance. The combination of celebrity talent and distinctive storytelling reflects Shinola's push to elevate its brand identity in the premium lifestyle space.
